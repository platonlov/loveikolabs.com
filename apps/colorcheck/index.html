ColorCheck is built for exactly this moment. Three seconds, real verdict, no guesswork.

The seven features that matter most

1. In-store BUY or SKIP verdict — the core job

Point your camera at any clothing item. Three seconds. "BUY IT — Olive Green, 87% match" or "SKIP IT — Mint Green, 23% match" with a one-sentence explanation of why and a suggested alternative ("Try a warm olive instead — same vibe, your colors"). This single feature is what makes ColorCheck different from the rest of the category. Every other app gives you a palette and stops there.

2. 12-season color analysis with confidence score

3-photo analysis (selfie + close-up of eyes + wrist) processed with multi-photo averaging. Result: your precise season from the professional 12-season system (Light Spring, True Spring, Warm Spring, Deep Spring, Soft Summer, etc.) with a confidence score. If confidence is below 70%, the app explicitly recommends a re-analysis with better lighting rather than committing you to an uncertain season. Other apps return binary "you are X" results with no transparency about certainty.

3. Savings counter — the financial ROI

Every SKIP scan in a store adds the price-tag value to your monthly savings counter. Home screen shows running totals: "Saved $341 this month." Most users see their savings exceed the annual subscription within the first 2-3 weeks. The counter creates visible ROI — wellness-tracking apps show streaks, ColorCheck shows dollars.

4. Closet audit — Day 1 engagement

After your season is determined, scan your existing closet. Each item gets a KEEP or DONATE verdict with reasoning. End of scan: "62% KEEP, 38% DONATE — $280 of wrong-color clothes." Most users scan 10-30 items the first day, generating dozens of moments where the app proves its value before the trial ends.

5. AI stylist chat for follow-up questions

"Does this neckline work for my shape?" "Can I wear gold jewelry with this?" "Is this shade too cool for my Warm Autumn palette?" The AI stylist is trained on the same color and styling knowledge base. Use it after a scan when you have questions the binary verdict didn't answer.

6. Celebrity color twins for social proof

"You're a Warm Autumn like Jessica Alba." "You're a Cool Winter like Anne Hathaway." Concrete celebrity references make the abstract season label aspirational and tangible. Solves the "I don't like my season" problem — being a Warm Autumn feels different when Jessica Alba is the example than when you're staring at a beige-and-brown palette.

7. 14-page PDF color report

Professional report equivalent to a $200-800 stylist session. Includes your season analysis with reasoning, full 30+ color palette with hex codes, best neutrals (your specific white, black, beige, navy, denim), metals guide (gold/silver/rose gold), celebrity matches, and a styling primer. Export and share with friends, save for reference, use as a stylist substitute for years.

How a ColorCheck session works

  1. Take 3 photos for your color season

    Selfie in natural daylight (window light, no filter, no makeup if possible). Close-up of your eyes. Close-up of your wrist (showing veins for undertone). Total time: under 2 minutes.

  2. Get your 12-season season with confidence score

    Result in 10-15 seconds: "You are a Warm Autumn (87% confidence). Your undertone is warm, your contrast is medium, your depth is medium-deep." Plus your 30+ color palette, celebrity matches, and best neutrals.

  3. Scan clothing items before you buy them

    In-store: hold the garment up, point camera, 3 seconds, verdict. Online: screenshot the product page or paste the URL (Phase 2), verdict with correction for known store filter biases.

  4. Track your savings monthly

    Every SKIP adds the avoided price to your counter. Optional: scan your existing closet for the audit feature, see which items to keep vs donate.
