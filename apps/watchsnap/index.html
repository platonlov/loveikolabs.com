What WatchSnap actually does

WatchSnap is an iOS app built on Apple's computer vision frameworks plus a watch-specific authentication model trained on reference imagery across brand-specific markers, super-clone signatures, and vintage patina patterns. Every scan returns six structured outputs:

  • Brand and model — Rolex Submariner 126610LN, Patek Nautilus 5711/1A, Omega Speedmaster Moonwatch — instant ID
  • Reference number and estimated year — exact movement reference plus production-year estimate from serial patterns
  • Market value range — current price band based on real market data from Chrono24, eBay sold, and WatchCharts trends
  • Authenticity verdict — Likely Genuine or Potential Replica with explicit confidence score (0-100%)
  • Genuine signs & warning signs — explicit list of what checked out and what raised flags (typography off, lume application inconsistent, date wheel font wrong, etc.)
  • 50+ inspection points analyzed — dial, bezel, crown, caseback, engravings, hour markers, hand alignment, lume color, date window magnification

The $500 super-clone problem: a modern factory-grade Rolex Submariner replica costs $500-$1,500 and visually fools expert collectors when handled in hand. The only consumer-grade defense is AI analysis of subtle markers the eye misses — typography on date wheels, lume color shift under different light, micro-engraving depth on rehauts. WatchSnap is built specifically for this defense layer.

How a WatchSnap scan works

  1. Open WatchSnap and tap scan

    Hold your iPhone 6-12 inches from the watch face. Good lighting matters — natural daylight or a bright neutral LED. Avoid harsh shadows.

  2. Capture 1-3 angles

    For best accuracy: a clear front (dial), a side profile (crown and lugs), and a caseback if accessible. The caseback is the highest-information surface for authentication — engraving depth and font are common super-clone failure points.

  3. Read the verdict in 3 seconds

    Brand, reference, year, market value range, and Likely Genuine / Potential Replica verdict with confidence score. Tap any warning sign for explanation. Tap "Ask the Expert" for follow-up questions. Save to Collection. Export PDF if needed.
