VeriBag — Frequently Asked Questions

Every question we hear most often about the 22-point handbag reference check, brand coverage, pricing, and when you actually need a physical authenticator instead of an AI tool.

How does VeriBag verify a handbag?

VeriBag uses an AI model trained on public reference material from sources like Bagaholic, Purseblog, Lollipuff archives, and Yoogi's Closet. You take or upload 6–8 guided photos — date code, stitching, hardware, glazing, heat stamp, interior tag, monogram at seam. The app compares each detail against 22 documented reference points for the specific brand and model you selected. Every checkpoint is marked consistent, flagged, or unclear, and every comparison cites the public source it's referencing. VeriBag is a reference checklist, not a certified authentication service.

Is VeriBag the same as Entrupy?

No. Entrupy is a microscopy-based authentication service that uses a proprietary handheld device to capture material-level images, paired with their own AI. Entrupy costs about $99 per individual consumer scan and issues authentication certificates backed by a financial guarantee. Since 2022 Entrupy has shifted toward business and consignment customers, making consumer access harder.

VeriBag uses your iPhone camera and a 22-point AI reference check — no microscopy, no certificate, no financial guarantee, $7.99 per week unlimited. Entrupy is the better tool when authentication needs to be legally defensible. VeriBag is the better tool for everyday pre-purchase sanity checks, pre-ship listing certificates, and bags under $5,000. Full comparison →

Which brands does VeriBag support?

VeriBag currently supports five brands: Louis Vuitton, Chanel, Hermès, Gucci, and Prada. These five brands dominate the secondhand luxury handbag market and account for the majority of pre-owned transactions on Vestiaire Collective, The RealReal, eBay, and Fashionphile. Each brand has its own 22-checkpoint pattern — for example, Chanel checkpoints include the hologram sticker and date code format, while Hermès checkpoints include the blind stamp font and saddle stitch density. Dior, Saint Laurent, Bottega Veneta, Celine, and Goyard are on the roadmap and will be added based on user demand and the depth of public reference material available.

Does VeriBag work for vintage handbags?

Partially. Bags produced from roughly 2000 onward have rich public reference documentation, and VeriBag's 22-checkpoint structure typically returns high-confidence verdicts. Pre-1990 vintage bags often return more 'unclear' checkpoints because public reference material thins out and craftsmanship variation across decades is significant. For vintage bags worth over $2,000 we recommend pairing VeriBag with a brand-specialist vintage authenticator like Bababebi (specializing in vintage Hermès) or a recognized vintage Chanel expert.

Can I use VeriBag for Vestiaire Collective, The RealReal, Poshmark, Depop, or Mercari?

Yes. VeriBag is built for secondhand luxury workflows on every major resale platform. Buyers use it before bidding to compare seller photos against the 22-checkpoint reference list — the app supports uploading existing listing photos, not just live camera capture. Resellers use it pre-ship to generate a shareable QR verify link they paste into the listing description.

The QR link takes the buyer to a public verify page at veribag.app/verify/{id} with the checkpoint breakdown — no app install required on the buyer's side. Sellers report fewer disputes because the buyer reviewed the 22 details before the sale, not after a freeze.

Does VeriBag guarantee authenticity?

No. VeriBag is an independent photo-based reference checklist. It does not issue certificates of authenticity, financial guarantees, or legally-defensible verdicts. Every checkpoint is marked consistent, flagged, or unclear with the public source it's compared against. For high-value transactions above $5,000, insurance claims, estate documentation, or legal disputes, we strongly recommend a certified physical authenticator — Entrupy, Bababebi, Real Authentication, or an in-store brand boutique inspection (note that some brands do not officially authenticate secondhand items).

How accurate is the 22-point reference check?

Accuracy depends on photo quality, the specific brand-model, and the era of production. Modern Louis Vuitton (post-2021 microchip generation) and Chanel (clear date codes, hologram stickers) typically reach high-confidence verdicts on most checkpoints. Hermès Birkin and Kelly are harder due to the depth of craftsmanship variation across decades and the long history of high-quality counterfeit production targeting these references. Vintage bags pre-1990 frequently return more 'unclear' checkpoints. VeriBag marks every checkpoint with consistent, flagged, or unclear — and never claims 100% accuracy. The 22-point structure is the same set of details a trained human authenticator examines first when handed a bag.

What should I do if VeriBag flags an authentic bag?

False flags happen. If you're holding a bag you bought directly from a brand boutique and VeriBag flags a checkpoint, the most common explanations are: photo quality issues (re-shoot the flagged angle with better lighting), an unusual production batch or year that diverges from the documented norm, or a less-documented model variant. The report shows you exactly which checkpoint flagged and the public source it was compared against — you can look at that source directly and decide. For high-stakes cases, a physical authenticator like Entrupy or Real Authentication will give you a more decisive answer. We are working on a 'second opinion' human review upsell for ambiguous cases.

Does VeriBag work for non-luxury brands like Coach or Michael Kors?

No. VeriBag specifically targets the five brands where secondhand authentication is a real financial problem — Louis Vuitton, Chanel, Hermès, Gucci, Prada. Coach and Michael Kors are counterfeited but the resale price gap between authentic and fake is small enough that a $7.99/wk verification often costs more than the price difference would save. If you need a verification tool for those brands, consider a generalist authentication service or the LegitApp generalist tier.

Can I use VeriBag for insurance documentation?

Not as primary evidence. Most homeowners and luxury-specific insurance policies require a written authentication letter or certificate from a recognized authentication service for claims above the policy's appraisal threshold. VeriBag's structured 22-point reference report is useful as supplementary documentation, but for the primary insurance record you should use Entrupy (authentication certificate) or Real Authentication (written authentication letter). VeriBag's PDF export can be attached to a policy file as additional evidence.

How does the shareable QR verify link work?

After a 22-point check completes, VeriBag generates a unique signed URL — for example veribag.app/verify/abc123. You can paste this URL or its QR code into any Poshmark, Depop, Vestiaire, eBay, or Mercari listing description. A buyer taps the link from their phone, lands on a branded verify page with the 22-checkpoint breakdown, the photos you submitted, and the timestamped report — no app install required on the buyer's side. The link is signed so it cannot be tampered with after generation. Resellers report meaningfully fewer disputes because buyers review the 22 details before purchase, not after a freeze claim.

Is my data private? Does VeriBag store my scan photos?

Scan photos are processed via a secure API proxy for the 30-second analysis and are not retained on our servers. Scan records — the text findings, the structured report, and any photos you choose to keep — live locally on your device. The public QR verify page only shows what you explicitly publish; you control which scans are shareable and which stay private. Full privacy policy at love8ko.github.io/veribag/privacy.html.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The Weekly plan ($7.99/wk) starts with a 7-day free trial that gives you unlimited 22-point checks across all five supported brands. The trial auto-converts to weekly billing unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the trial ends. The Annual and Lifetime plans do not include trials but are positioned as upgrade tiers after you've tested the product.

How do I cancel my VeriBag subscription?

All cancellation is handled natively by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name at the top → tap Subscriptions → select VeriBag → tap Cancel Subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period and your remaining time stays active. Loveiko Labs has no direct subscription portal — Apple manages everything to keep refunds and billing fully under your control. For refund requests within Apple's 7-day window, use the 'Report a Problem' flow in Apple's billing history.

Does VeriBag work without internet?

No. The 22-point AI reference check runs on a cloud-based model, so an internet connection is required for new scans. Past scan reports stored on your device remain accessible offline. The QR verify pages are hosted on our servers and require internet for buyers to view. For offline pre-purchase situations (estate sales with no signal), we recommend capturing all 6–8 photos in the app, then running the scan when you regain a connection — usually before you finalize the purchase.

How is VeriBag different from a $5 LegitApp generalist check?

LegitApp is a generalist multi-category authentication tool that covers sneakers, watches, handbags, electronics, and more — broad but shallow per category. VeriBag focuses exclusively on five luxury handbag brands and goes deep: 22 documented checkpoints per brand-model, era-specific reference patterns (like the 2021+ Louis Vuitton microchip generation), and source citations to public reference guides. LegitApp is a good fit if you need to check many different categories occasionally. VeriBag is a better fit when secondhand luxury handbags are the recurring category, especially for resellers and pre-bid buyers on Vestiaire or The RealReal.

Can resellers integrate VeriBag with Poshmark, Depop, Mercari, or Vestiaire APIs?

Direct platform API integration is on our roadmap for the Lifetime tier. The current workflow is manual: run a 22-point check, copy the QR verify URL, paste it into your listing description. Resellers running 50+ listings per week have asked for bulk export and listing automation; we're scoping that for an upgrade path. For now, the manual paste-link workflow handles every major platform without needing platform-specific approvals.

What if I need a written authentication letter for a legal dispute?

VeriBag does not produce written authentication letters. For legal disputes, insurance claims, or any case where you need a defensible third-party verdict, use Real Authentication (written authenticator letters, $25–60 per item) or Entrupy (authentication certificate with financial guarantee, ~$99 per scan). VeriBag's structured 22-point reference report can be useful as supplementary evidence — it shows you did due diligence — but it is not a substitute for a written professional authentication.

Why does Entrupy cost $99 per bag if VeriBag is $7.99 per week unlimited?

Entrupy uses a proprietary microscopy device that captures material-level surface detail your iPhone camera cannot reach, plus a financial guarantee that requires capital reserves to back. VeriBag uses your iPhone camera to compare visible photo details against a 22-point public reference pattern — no microscopy, no financial guarantee, and the AI model amortizes across many users. This is why we position VeriBag as a reference checklist rather than a certified service. Entrupy is the right tool when the verdict needs to hold up in a dispute. VeriBag is the right tool for everyday checks.
