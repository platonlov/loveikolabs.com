What Calibrum Is

Calibrum is an educational companion app for adult men with clinically diagnosed low testosterone who are managing prescribed TRT under physician supervision. It is not a medical device. It does not prescribe medication, recommend dose changes, or replace consultation with your urologist, endocrinologist, or primary care provider. It is a tool that sits between your quarterly appointments — when you are alone with your hormones and your prescriber is not available — and surfaces educational context grounded in clinical guidelines so the conversation with your provider is more informed, not replaced.

What it is not. Calibrum is not built for bodybuilders, enhanced athletes, off-label cycling, or grey-market testosterone sourcing. The AI Hormone Coach is trained on publicly available content from the Endocrine Society, the American Urological Association (AUA), AAFP TRT monographs, and peer-reviewed clinical research — not bodybuilding forums or enhanced-athlete research. If you are running a non-medical protocol, there are other apps for that.

The Six Things Calibrum Does That Trackers Don't

1. AI Hormone Coach (24/7)

Wondering if your libido drop is from high E2 or something else? Bloated and not sure why? Joint pain on week six? Ask the coach. Every answer cites the underlying guideline (Endocrine Society 2018 clinical practice guidelines, AUA testosterone evaluation/management algorithms, AAFP TRT monographs). Plain English. No broscience. No guesswork. The coach surfaces what publicly available clinical guidelines say — it does not diagnose, prescribe, or recommend dose changes.

2. Lab Snap and Decode (Killer Feature)

Photograph your LabCorp or Quest PDF. On-device OCR plus AI vision extracts every marker: Total Testosterone, Free Testosterone, SHBG, Estradiol, Hematocrit, Hemoglobin, PSA, and more. Review every value before saving. Calibrum then plots trends over time with reference range bands and surfaces what each marker typically means in the context of TRT — based on the clinical guidelines, not a generic "normal" range from the lab's printout.

3. E2 Symptom Triage

The most underserved part of TRT. Mood off? Libido down? Joint pain? Nipple sensitivity? Bloating? The AI pattern-matches your symptoms against published high-E2 vs low-E2 vs unrelated profiles, weighted against your most recent E2 lab value and estimated medication timing curve. The output is a triage summary you can take to your prescriber — not a recommendation to adjust anastrozole or your testosterone dose yourself.

4. Doctor Visit Prep PDF

Before each urology or endocrinology appointment, Calibrum auto-generates a PDF summary covering the last 90 days: dose history, symptom trends, lab summaries with trend charts, and three to five personalized questions to ask your prescriber. Walk into the appointment with a one-page brief, not a vague "I don't feel great." This alone has changed appointment quality for many users.

5. Free Testosterone Calculator (Free Tier)

Calculate Free T and Bioavailable T using the Vermeulen formula — the gold standard equation derived from your Total T, SHBG, and albumin lab values. See whether your numbers fall within typical optimal ranges for your age. Permanently free, no subscription required. We put this in the free tier because Free T calculation is essential information and should not be locked behind a paywall.

6. Smart Bloodwork Timing

Changed your testosterone dose six weeks ago? Started anastrozole at 0.25mg every other day? Calibrum calculates when your levels likely stabilize based on the medication's published half-life and recommends when to retest. Direct order links for at-home labs available where supported. Stop guessing whether your next blood draw is too early to be meaningful.

Supported Medications and Protocols

Calibrum supports the medications and routes typically used in clinical TRT in the United States:

  • Testosterone esters (injectable). Cypionate (commonly known as Depo-Testosterone®), enanthate, propionate, and the long-acting undecanoate (Aveed®). IM weekly, twice-weekly, SubQ daily, or alternate-day protocols.
  • Testosterone topical. Gels including AndroGel®, Testim®, Fortesta®.
  • Testosterone pellets. Testopel® and compounded equivalents.
  • Oral testosterone. Jatenzo® (testosterone undecanoate, Clarus Therapeutics), Kyzatrex® (Marius Pharmaceuticals).
  • Ancillary medications. Anastrozole (Arimidex® and generics), exemestane (Aromasin®), hCG, enclomiphene, and other ancillaries commonly used alongside TRT under physician supervision.

Privacy

TRT is sensitive medical data. Calibrum is built for men who do not want their hormone levels, libido scores, or lab values on a stranger's server.

  • No account required. Open the app and start using it. No email, no phone number, no name.
  • All data stays on your device. Doses, lab values, symptom logs, photos of lab reports. None of it leaves your phone.
  • Lab Snap uses on-device OCR. Text extraction happens locally where possible. When AI vision is needed for complex PDFs, only the cropped marker region is routed through a privacy proxy and is never stored or linked to your identity.
  • No data sold. No ads. No analytics tied to identity.
  • Free Testosterone Calculator works without subscription and without sending any data anywhere. Enter your numbers, get the calculation, done.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Calibrum tell me whether to raise or lower my testosterone dose?

No. Calibrum does not recommend dose changes under any circumstances. That decision belongs to you and your prescribing physician. What Calibrum does do is surface trend patterns, symptom-vs-lab correlations, and the clinical-guideline context for what your prescriber is likely to consider when evaluating your protocol. The output is meant to make your next appointment more productive — not to replace it.

Does the AI Hormone Coach replace my doctor?

No. The AI Hormone Coach is an educational reference layer, not a medical provider. It cites the same clinical guidelines your endocrinologist or urologist references but it is not licensed to diagnose, prescribe, or treat. For any actual medical decision — starting TRT, adjusting dose, adding anastrozole, stopping for fertility considerations, addressing erythrocytosis — consult your prescriber.

What about cardiovascular and prostate risks?

These are real considerations on TRT and Calibrum surfaces published guidance on both. Hematocrit above 54% is a documented thrombotic risk threshold and the AI Coach flags it with the standard guideline reference. PSA trend monitoring is built into the Lab Snap workflow with reference to AUA prostate surveillance protocols. For any concerning value — significant hematocrit elevation, PSA velocity spike, chest pain, severe edema — call your prescriber or 911 immediately, regardless of what any app shows.

Does Calibrum work for transgender men or gender-affirming hormone therapy?

Calibrum's clinical guidance content is currently built around male hypogonadism management as defined in the Endocrine Society and AUA guidelines. It can be used for injection tracking, lab tracking, and the Free T calculator in any context, but the E2 symptom triage and clinical-context features are framed for cis male TRT. For gender-affirming care apps with community and milestone tracking, Transcapsule and similar community-focused apps may serve those use cases better.

Is the $49.99 annual subscription a one-time charge or recurring?

Recurring. It auto-renews annually unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. The 7-day free trial converts to paid if you do not cancel before the trial ends. Manage in iPhone Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions. The Free Testosterone Calculator works permanently in the free tier without any subscription.

What happens when I cancel my subscription?

You keep access until the end of the current billing period. After that, the AI Hormone Coach, Lab Snap, E2 Triage, Doctor Visit Prep, and Smart Bloodwork Timing become unavailable. The Free Testosterone Calculator continues to work. Your local data stays on your device and you can re-subscribe at any time to regain access.

Important Disclaimer

Calibrum is an educational companion app. It is not a medical device. It does not prescribe medication, recommend dose changes, diagnose conditions, or replace consultation with your prescribing physician. The AI Hormone Coach provides educational information based on publicly available clinical guidelines (Endocrine Society, AUA, AAFP) — not personalized medical advice. Do not adjust your testosterone dose, anastrozole dose, hCG dose, or any other medication based on app output without consulting your prescriber.

Seek emergency care immediately for:

  • Severe chest pain, shortness of breath, or signs of stroke — call 911
  • Severe testicular pain or sudden testicular swelling — emergency department
  • Signs of a blood clot (sudden leg swelling/pain, severe headache, vision changes) — call 911
  • Severe allergic reaction to any medication — call 911
  • Hematocrit above 54% on bloodwork — contact your prescriber immediately (donate blood or pause dose only on prescriber's instruction)
  • Significant PSA elevation flagged by your lab — contact your urologist
  • Mental health crisis or suicidal thoughts — call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline)

Testosterone is a Schedule III controlled substance in the United States. Calibrum is built exclusively for patients managing legally prescribed TRT under physician supervision. We do not support, normalize, or provide guidance for unprescribed testosterone use, grey-market sourcing, or off-label bodybuilding cycles.
