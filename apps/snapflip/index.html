Overview

What is SnapFlip?

SnapFlip is an iOS thrift scanner built for resellers. It connects to the eBay developer API to show real completed sale prices — not AI guesses — for anything you point your camera at.

Every scan returns nine structured outputs in under three seconds: Flip Score (1-100), BUY/SKIP verdict, brand and item details, real eBay sold prices, multi-platform pricing across eBay/Mercari/Poshmark, sales velocity, profit calculator, AI listing generator, and Flip Collection portfolio tracking.

The key difference from every other thrift scanner app: other apps show AI-estimated prices that can be off by 30-50% from reality. SnapFlip shows actual completed sale prices from the eBay API. A current listing is what someone hopes to get. A sold listing is what someone actually got. That gap is often 30-50% — and it is the gap that turns a profitable flip into a money-losing dud sitting in your garage for six months.

Every price has a source badge. Real sold data or AI estimate — you always know which number you're looking at. Tap "See on eBay" to verify any price against live completed listings in seconds.

Capabilities

Everything SnapFlip does — nine outputs in three seconds

  • Flip Score (1-100) — a numerical buy/skip verdict calculated from profit margin, demand signals, and sell-through rate. 80+ is green (BUY), 40-79 is yellow (check details), under 40 is red (SKIP). Unlike binary verdicts, the score lets you make nuanced decisions at the rack.
  • Real eBay sold prices via official eBay API — actual completed sales from the last 30 days, not aspirational asking prices or training-data estimates. Source badge on every price. Tap any figure to open the live eBay listing and verify it yourself.
  • Multi-platform pricing — eBay, Mercari, Poshmark side-by-side — the same item can sell for 30-40% more on the right platform. eBay for electronics and vintage, Poshmark for women's branded clothing, Mercari for everyday fast movers. SnapFlip shows all three so you route correctly at the source.
  • Sales velocity — real sold count over last 30 days — "23 sold in 30 days" tells you the item moves weekly. "3 sold" means it will sit in inventory for months. This is a concrete number, not a vague "high demand" label.
  • Profit calculator with platform fees and shipping — enter your buy price and see net profit after eBay's 13% final value fee, Mercari's 10% + 2.9%, or Poshmark's 20% flat (or $2.95 under $15). No mental math while you're standing in the aisle.
  • AI listing generator — one tap produces a platform-optimized title, condition-matched description, and data-backed suggested price. Copy to clipboard, open eBay/Mercari/Poshmark, paste, post. Under 90 seconds from scan to live listing.
  • Brand and item detail recognition — clothing, sneakers, electronics, toys, books, vintage items, collectibles, jewelry, kitchen goods, accessories, tools. Best results when brand tag or model number is visible. Supported by Apple Vision plus a custom resale-trained AI model.
  • Flip Collection portfolio — save every scan, track buy prices, monitor total portfolio value. Sort by Flip Score or filter by category. CSV export available for Pro subscribers — feeds straight into tax-season accounting.
  • Refine Analysis — if the initial scan confidence is low, add a second photo (especially the brand tag) or type the item manually. Scan re-runs at no extra cost. High-confidence corrections can be emailed directly to the team to improve the AI model.

Categories worth scanning in 2026

Not everything in a thrift store is worth your scan time. Here is where SnapFlip reliably earns its keep — and where to skip without scanning.

  • Reliable winners — almost always worth scanning: vintage band tees (1990s grunge, hip-hop, metal — plain-looking shirts can flip for $80-200), Patagonia / North Face / Arc'teryx outerwear, designer denim (Levi's selvedge, vintage 501s), vintage Pyrex and Le Creuset (specific pattern names matter), vintage electronics (Sony Walkmans, vintage cameras, gaming systems), vintage jewelry.
  • Marginal — scan only if priced very low: modern fast-fashion (H&M, Zara, Forever 21), generic home decor, modern post-2010 toys without specific collectibility, books except first editions or specialty titles.
  • Skip without scanning: damaged items (stains, tears, missing parts — return rates kill margins), bulky furniture (shipping costs eat all profit), items with no brand information at all, and counterfeit-prone categories at suspiciously low prices.

Questions

Frequently asked questions — all 20

What makes SnapFlip different from other thrift scanner apps?

SnapFlip is the only consumer thrift scanner that connects directly to the eBay API for real completed sale prices. Other apps show AI-estimated prices that can be off by 30-50% from reality. Every price in SnapFlip carries a source badge — real sold data or AI estimate — so you always know what you're looking at. Tap "See on eBay" to verify any price against live completed listings.

How accurate is the Flip Score?

The Flip Score (1-100) combines three signals: profit margin (your potential buy price vs recent sold prices), demand (sold count in last 30 days), and sell-through rate (how often the item moves vs sits unsold). 80+ is a clear BUY. 40-79 is a maybe. Under 40 is SKIP. Most accurate on branded items with active listings; obscure items show lower confidence.

What does "real eBay sold prices" actually mean?

It means the prices come from completed eBay listings — items that actually sold, not items currently listed at aspirational prices. SnapFlip pulls this from the eBay developer API. Other apps either scrape current asking prices (which are usually higher than what items actually sell for) or generate AI estimates from training data. Real sold data is the only number that matters when you're deciding what to pay at a thrift store.

Why are sold prices more useful than current listings?

A current listing is what someone hopes to get. A sold listing is what someone actually got. The gap is often 30-50%. Example: a Patagonia fleece might be listed at $120 by ten sellers, but actual completed sales over the last 30 days range from $65-95. The $120 number is irrelevant to your sourcing decision. SnapFlip shows you the $65-95 number.

How does SnapFlip identify what an item is?

Apple Vision framework plus a custom AI model trained on resale-relevant categories. Best results when the brand tag, model number, or maker mark is visible. For clothing, photograph the brand tag. For sneakers, photograph the tongue label. For electronics, photograph the serial sticker. For vintage items, capture any maker marks. If uncertain, use Refine Analysis to add another photo or type the item manually.

Does SnapFlip work on clothing, sneakers, electronics, books?

Yes — plus toys, home goods, vintage items, collectibles, jewelry, accessories, kitchen items, and tools. Best results on items with clear branding visible. Unbranded or generic items work but with lower-confidence scores.

Does SnapFlip handle vintage and antique items?

Yes. Vintage clothing (designer vintage, denim, band tees), mid-century home goods, vintage electronics, antique books, and collectibles all work. Era recognition is approximate — for fine antiques requiring deep expertise, consult a specialist.

Can SnapFlip authenticate luxury items?

SnapFlip identifies items but is not built for authentication. For luxury handbags, Loveiko Labs makes VeriBag — a separate app with 22-point photo authentication. For luxury watches, WatchSnap. For fine jewelry, JewelSnap. If SnapFlip identifies an item as a high-value luxury piece, the verdict will recommend professional authentication before purchase.

What if the scan identifies an item incorrectly?

Use the Refine Analysis feature — add a clearer photo (especially of the brand tag), or type the item manually. The scan re-runs with corrected input at no extra scan cost.

Does SnapFlip work without internet?

No. Internet is required because both AI identification and eBay sold-price lookup happen in real time via cloud APIs. On a typical 4G/5G connection, scan-to-verdict completes in under 3 seconds. Saved scans in your Flip Collection can be viewed offline.

How does multi-platform pricing work?

For every scanned item, SnapFlip shows side-by-side average sale prices on eBay, Mercari, and Poshmark — so you know where the same item sells for most. Generally: eBay pays best for electronics and vintage, Poshmark pays best for women's branded clothing, Mercari sells fastest for everyday items. The app shows the data so you can pick the right platform for each item.

How is the profit calculator different from just subtracting fees?

It accounts for all three platforms' actual fee structures and shipping. eBay: 13% final value fee plus payment processing. Mercari: 10% plus 2.9% payment processing. Poshmark: 20% flat (items over $15) or $2.95 (items under $15) plus shipping costs. Enter buy price and platform, see net profit after all fees and shipping. Saves the mental math when you're making fast decisions in a store.

What's the AI listing generator and how does it save time?

One tap produces a complete listing: title (optimized for the chosen platform's search algorithm), description (matching identified condition, brand, materials), and suggested price (based on multi-platform data). Copy to clipboard, paste in eBay/Mercari/Poshmark app, post. Saves 15-20 minutes per listing — significant when listing 30 items from a single thrift haul.

How fresh is the eBay sold price data?

Sold prices reflect the most recent completed listings — typically updated within hours of actual sale. The sales velocity metric shows count over the last 30 days. For trending items (sneaker releases, viral products) prices change weekly. For stable categories (vintage jewelry, mid-century furniture) prices are stable over months.

Is SnapFlip affiliated with eBay, Mercari, or Poshmark?

No. SnapFlip uses publicly available data and the official eBay developer API to fetch sold prices. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated in partnership with eBay, Mercari, Poshmark, Depop, Vinted, or Goodwill.

What's a good Flip Score to buy at?

General rule: 80+ is a confident BUY (good margin, active demand). 60-79 is worth considering if your buy price is well below the lower end of the sold range. 40-59 is marginal — only worth it for very cheap finds or items you'd keep anyway. Under 40 is SKIP unless you have specialty knowledge the AI lacks.

How is SnapFlip different from googling "eBay sold" on a thrift run?

Speed and completeness. Googling each item takes 5-15 minutes per piece: identify it, find the right eBay search, filter to sold listings, scan results, do mental math on fees. SnapFlip does all of this in 3 seconds plus shows Mercari and Poshmark comps simultaneously. On a thrift run with 50 candidate items, that's the difference between getting through 5 items and getting through all 50.

What's the difference between weekly and annual subscription?

Both unlock identical features. Weekly is $4.99/week with a 7-day free trial — best for short-term needs (one estate sale weekend, occasional thrifting). Annual is $29.99/year — saves 88% versus weekly billing. Best for serious resellers who source weekly. Cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Cancellation takes effect at end of current billing period. The 7-day free trial can be cancelled any time before it ends with no charge.

Can I export my Flip Collection for tax purposes?

Yes. Flip Collection saves every scan with buy price tracking. You can view total portfolio value, sort by Flip Score, filter by category. CSV export is available for Pro subscribers — useful for tax season if reselling is a business. Includes scan date, identified item, buy price, sold-price range, and platform recommendation for each entry.
