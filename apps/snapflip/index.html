What SnapFlip actually does

SnapFlip is an iOS thrift scanner built for resellers. Every scan returns nine structured outputs in under three seconds:

  • Flip Score (1-100) — 80+ buy, 40-79 maybe, under 40 skip
  • BUY or SKIP verdict — AI decision based on profit potential, demand, and sell-through rate
  • Brand and item details — brand, model, material, category, condition
  • Real eBay sold prices — actual completed sales from the eBay API, tap "See on eBay" to verify
  • Multi-platform pricing — eBay, Mercari, Poshmark side-by-side so you know where it pays most
  • Sales velocity — concrete count, "23 sold in 30 days," not a vague "high demand" label
  • Profit calculator — enter your buy price, see net profit after platform fees (13% eBay, 10% Mercari, 20% Poshmark) and shipping
  • AI listing generator — one tap produces title, description, suggested price, copy-paste to your chosen platform
  • Flip Collection — save every scan, track buy prices, monitor your portfolio value

The key difference: other thrift scanner apps show AI-estimated prices that can be off by 30-50% from reality. SnapFlip shows the actual completed sale prices from the eBay API. Every price has a source badge — real sold data or AI estimate — so you always know what you're looking at before you buy.

Garage sale and estate sale hunters

Estate sale opens at 8 AM. Twenty flippers in line. First hour is chaos and you can't price-check on Google fast enough. SnapFlip is built for the speed advantage — three seconds per item, batch through a hundred pieces before lunch.

Flea market resellers

You triage dozens of items per visit. Some are obvious yes-or-no. Most are unclear. SnapFlip's confidence-scored Flip Score lets you sort through a pile of stuff in minutes rather than hours.

Poshmark, Mercari, and eBay sellers

You're not sourcing from thrift stores — you're selling what you already own (closet cleanout, kids outgrew it, downsizing). SnapFlip's AI listing generator turns each item into a copy-paste-ready listing with title, description, and suggested price in one tap. Saves 15-20 minutes per item.

Beginners new to thrifting

Saw a TikTok about someone making $500/week flipping. Walked into Goodwill, bewildered. SnapFlip works as an expert in your pocket — point the camera, learn what's worth buying and what isn't. You build intuition fast by watching the AI's reasoning.

How a SnapFlip scan works

Three steps from camera to verdict, optimized for the speed you need on a sourcing trip.

  1. Point camera at the item and tap scan

    Hold the iPhone 6-12 inches away. SnapFlip prompts you if lighting or focus is insufficient. For clothing, capture the brand tag if visible — boosts accuracy significantly.

  2. Read the verdict in under 3 seconds

    Flip Score lights up at the top (green 80+, yellow 40-79, red <40). Below: real eBay sold prices with source badges, multi-platform comparison, sales velocity. Tap any price to open it on eBay.

  3. If buying, tap "Generate Listing"

    One tap produces title, description, and suggested price. Copy to clipboard, open eBay/Mercari/Poshmark app, paste, and post. Total time from photo to live listing: under 60 seconds.

The math of reselling with SnapFlip

One good flip per week pays for the annual subscription ten times over.

Concrete example: a $6 Patagonia fleece at Goodwill. Without SnapFlip you'd Google for ten minutes or buy on instinct. With SnapFlip in three seconds: Flip Score 87, real eBay comps showing $75-95 recent sells, 18 sold in last 30 days. You buy for $6, list for $89, pay $11.57 in eBay fees (13%), and net $77.43 profit after fees. One flip recovers the annual subscription nearly three times over.

The flip-side example: a Coach handbag at a flea market for $40. Looks vintage, looks real, instinct says buy. SnapFlip scan: Flip Score 22, real comps showing $35-55 average sell on similar bags, but only 3 sold in 30 days (sales velocity low). Verdict: SKIP. You saved $40 from sitting in inventory for six months.
