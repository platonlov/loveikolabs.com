How SnapFlip works

1. What makes SnapFlip different from other thrift scanner apps?

SnapFlip is the only consumer thrift scanner that connects directly to the eBay API for real completed sale prices. Other apps show AI-estimated prices that can be off by 30-50% from reality.

Every price in SnapFlip carries a source badge — real sold data or AI estimate — so you always know what you're looking at. Tap "See on eBay" to verify any price against live completed listings.

2. How accurate is the Flip Score?

The Flip Score (1-100) combines three signals:

  • Profit margin — your potential buy price vs recent sold prices
  • Demand — sold count in last 30 days
  • Sell-through rate — how often the item moves vs sits unsold

80+ is a clear BUY. 40-79 is a maybe. Under 40 is SKIP. Most accurate on branded items with active listings; obscure items show lower confidence.

3. What does "real eBay sold prices" actually mean?

It means the prices come from completed eBay listings — items that actually sold, not items currently listed at aspirational prices. SnapFlip pulls this from the eBay developer API.

Other apps either scrape current asking prices (which are usually higher than what items actually sell for) or generate AI estimates from training data. Real sold data is the only number that matters when you're deciding what to pay at a thrift store.

4. Why are sold prices more useful than current listings?

A current listing is what someone hopes to get. A sold listing is what someone actually got. The gap is often 30-50%.

Example: a Patagonia fleece might be listed at $120 by ten sellers, but actual completed sales over the last 30 days range from $65-95. The $120 number is irrelevant to your sourcing decision. SnapFlip shows you the $65-95 number.

5. How does SnapFlip identify what an item is?

Apple Vision framework plus a custom AI model trained on resale-relevant categories. Best results when the brand tag, model number, or maker mark is visible.

  • For clothing — photograph the brand tag
  • For sneakers — photograph the tongue label
  • For electronics — photograph the serial sticker
  • For vintage — capture any maker marks

If uncertain, use Refine Analysis to add another photo or type the item manually.

What it works on

6. Does SnapFlip work on clothing, sneakers, electronics, books?

Yes — plus toys, home goods, vintage items, collectibles, jewelry, accessories, kitchen items, and tools. Best results on items with clear branding visible. Unbranded or generic items work but with lower-confidence scores.

7. Does SnapFlip handle vintage and antique items?

Yes. Vintage clothing (designer vintage, denim, band tees), mid-century home goods, vintage electronics, antique books, and collectibles all work. Era recognition is approximate — for fine antiques requiring expertise, take to a specialist.

8. Can SnapFlip authenticate luxury items?

SnapFlip identifies items but is not built for authentication. For luxury handbags, Loveiko Labs makes VeriBag (22-point photo authentication). For luxury watches, WatchSnap. For fine jewelry, JewelSnap.

If SnapFlip identifies an item as high-value luxury, the verdict will recommend professional authentication before purchase.

9. What if the scan identifies an item incorrectly?

Use Refine Analysis — add a clearer photo (especially the brand tag), or type the item manually. The scan re-runs with corrected input at no extra scan cost.

If high-confidence scans are consistently wrong, email loveykovl@gmail.com with the photo — this feedback improves the AI directly.

10. Does SnapFlip work without internet?

No. Internet is required because both AI identification and eBay sold-price lookup happen in real time via cloud APIs.

On a typical 4G/5G connection, scan-to-verdict completes in under 3 seconds. Saved scans in your Flip Collection can be viewed offline.

Pricing and platforms

11. How does multi-platform pricing work?

For every scanned item, SnapFlip shows side-by-side average sale prices on eBay, Mercari, and Poshmark. Generally:

  • eBay pays best for electronics, vintage, collectibles
  • Poshmark pays best for women's branded clothing
  • Mercari sells fastest for everyday items

The app shows the data so you can pick the right platform for each item.

12. How is the profit calculator different from just subtracting fees?

It accounts for all three platforms' actual fee structures and shipping:

  • eBay: 13% final value fee + PayPal/payment processing
  • Mercari: 10% + 2.9% payment processing
  • Poshmark: 20% flat (items over $15) or $2.95 (under $15) + shipping

Enter buy price and platform — see net profit after all fees and shipping. Saves the mental math when making fast decisions in a store.

13. What's the AI listing generator?

One tap produces a complete resale listing:

  • Title — optimized for the chosen platform's search algorithm
  • Description — matching identified condition, brand, materials
  • Suggested price — based on multi-platform data

Copy to clipboard → paste in eBay/Mercari/Poshmark app → post. Saves 15-20 minutes per listing. Significant when listing 30 items from a single thrift haul.

14. How fresh is the eBay sold price data?

Sold prices reflect the most recent completed listings — typically updated within hours of actual sale. The sales velocity metric shows count over last 30 days.

For trending items (sneaker releases, viral products) prices change weekly. For stable categories (vintage jewelry, mid-century furniture) prices are stable over months.

15. Is SnapFlip affiliated with eBay, Mercari, or Poshmark?

No. SnapFlip uses publicly available data and the official eBay developer API to fetch sold prices. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated in partnership with eBay, Mercari, Poshmark, Depop, Vinted, or Goodwill.

Strategy and pricing

16. What's a good Flip Score to buy at?

General rules:

  • 80+ — confident BUY (good margin, active demand)
  • 60-79 — worth considering if buy price is well below lower end of sold range
  • 40-59 — marginal, only for very cheap finds or items you'd keep anyway
  • Under 40 — SKIP unless you have specialty knowledge the AI lacks
17. How is SnapFlip different from googling "eBay sold" on a thrift run?

Speed and completeness.

Googling each item takes 5-15 minutes per piece: identify it, find right search, filter to sold, scan results, do mental math on fees. SnapFlip does all of this in 3 seconds plus shows Mercari and Poshmark comps simultaneously.

On a thrift run with 50 candidate items, that's the difference between getting through 5 items and getting through all 50.

19. How do I cancel SnapFlip Pro?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. To cancel:

  1. Open the Settings app
  2. Tap your Apple ID at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Find SnapFlip → tap Cancel Subscription

Cancellation takes effect at end of current billing period. The 7-day free trial can be cancelled any time before it ends with no charge.

20. Can I export my Flip Collection for tax purposes?

Yes. Flip Collection saves every scan with buy price tracking. You can view total portfolio value, sort by Flip Score, filter by category.

CSV export is available for Pro subscribers — useful for tax season if reselling is a business. Includes scan date, identified item, buy price, sold-price range, and platform recommendation for each entry.
