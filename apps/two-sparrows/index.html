Overview

What is Two Sparrows?

Two Sparrows is a Bible verse widget for iPhone. It puts a new verse on your Lock Screen every morning, adds Home Screen widgets in three sizes and a StandBy view for your nightstand, and helps you find verses for how you feel. Every word comes from the Berean Standard Bible.

Most of us pick up the phone dozens of times a day. Two Sparrows puts Scripture on the screen you already look at, sized so the whole verse fits, not a cut-off line. The larger widgets let you listen, save, share or get another verse without opening the app.

The name comes from Matthew 10:31: "So do not be afraid; you are worth more than many sparrows." The app is built to be calm: no ads, no feed, no chat and no streaks. Missed a day? Nothing resets.

What Two Sparrows does not do. It does not write Scripture with AI. Every verse is from the Berean Standard Bible, and the short notes under "About this verse" come from the Two Sparrows editors. AI only helps match verses to how you feel or what you type, and the "Scripture only" setting hides every AI feature. Two Sparrows is not a church, a pastor or a counselor.

Capabilities

What Two Sparrows includes

  • Lock Screen verse. A new Bible verse every morning, the full verse, sized to fit. You can also pin a saved verse to your Lock Screen.
  • Home Screen widgets in three sizes. The larger widgets have Listen, Save, Share and Another verse buttons right on the widget.
  • StandBy. A nightstand view with a calm Candle look at night. Looks to choose from: Linen, Candle, Paper, Plain Dark and large text.
  • Guided setup. Add the Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets in one guided pass, with a short video for each step.
  • A verse for how you feel. Tap anxious, afraid, grieving, can't sleep, lonely, weary or grateful, or type or speak your own words, and get fitting verses from the Scripture library. Every verse opens in its chapter, with related verses to keep reading.
  • 7-day paths. Peace for anxious hearts, Trust when you're afraid, Comfort in grief, Sleep in peace, You are not alone, Start the day with God, 7 Psalms to pray, and Words of Jesus.
  • Listen and pray. Verses and prayers read aloud, word for word. Saved verses play with a sleep timer, and an evening prayer arrives at the time you choose, the only reminder the app sends.
  • The whole Bible, offline. The full Berean Standard Bible is on your iPhone and works without a connection. Share any verse as a picture in Story, Square or Wallpaper format.

Workflow

How Two Sparrows works — your first week

The free week is designed to become a habit you keep without trying, not a plan you fall behind on.

  1. Day 1 — set up your widgets.A guided pass adds the Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets, with a short video for each step. Pick a look: Linen, Candle, Paper, Plain Dark or large text.
  2. Every morning — a new verse.Your Lock Screen shows a new verse, the full text, sized to fit. Want a different one? Tap Another verse on the larger widget.
  3. When you need it — a verse for how you feel.Tap a feeling or say what's on your mind, and read verses that fit. Each opens in its chapter with related verses.
  4. Along the way — a 7-day path.One verse, one prayer and one small step a day. Listen instead of reading if you prefer, and set the time for your evening prayer.
  5. Day 7 — decide.The weekly plan starts on day 7 unless you cancel in Settings at least 24 hours before. Cancel in time and you pay nothing.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Two Sparrows?

Two Sparrows is a Bible verse widget for iPhone. It shows a new verse of the day on your Lock Screen, offers Home Screen widgets in three sizes and a StandBy view, and helps you find verses for how you feel. Every word comes from the Berean Standard Bible.

Which Bible translation does Two Sparrows use?

The Berean Standard Bible (BSB), a modern English translation in the public domain. The whole BSB is stored on your iPhone, so it works offline.

Does Two Sparrows use AI to write verses?

No. There are no AI-written words in the Scripture. AI only helps match verses to how you feel or to what you type or say. If you prefer none at all, turn on "Scripture only" and every AI feature is hidden.

How do I put a Bible verse on my iPhone Lock Screen?

Two Sparrows walks you through it. A guided setup adds the Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets in one pass, with a short video for each step. After that, a new verse appears on your Lock Screen every morning.

Does it work with StandBy?

Yes. When your iPhone is charging on its side, StandBy shows your verse, with a calm Candle look at night.

Can I listen instead of reading?

Yes. Verses and prayers are read aloud, word for word, and you can play your saved verses with a sleep timer. The larger Home Screen widgets have a Listen button.

Are there ads, streaks or a social feed?

No. Two Sparrows has no ads, no feed, no chat and no streaks. If you miss a day of a 7-day path, nothing resets. The only reminder it sends is an evening prayer at the time you choose.

How does the free week work?

New subscribers on the weekly plan get 7 days free with every feature. On day 7 the weekly plan starts unless you cancel in Settings at least 24 hours before. If you cancel in time, you pay nothing.

How much does Two Sparrows cost?

In the US, Two Sparrows Pro is $7.99 per week after a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, or $39.99 per year with no free trial. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Which languages is Two Sparrows available in?

The App Store lists English, French, German, Portuguese and Spanish. Two Sparrows needs iOS 17.0 or later.

About Scripture. All Scripture in Two Sparrows is from the Berean Standard Bible, which is in the public domain. The short notes under "About this verse" are written by the Two Sparrows editors and are not part of Scripture.

About AI. AI is used only to suggest verses that fit a feeling or the words you type or say. Like any AI, its suggestions can miss; the verses themselves are always quoted word for word. "Scripture only" turns every AI feature off.

Not pastoral or medical care. Two Sparrows is a devotional tool. It is not a substitute for your church, a pastor, a counselor or medical care. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 in the US or your local emergency number.
