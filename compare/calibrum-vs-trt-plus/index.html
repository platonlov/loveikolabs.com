Calibrum vs TRT Plus

An honest head-to-head of two TRT companion apps. We make Calibrum and disclose that here.

Both apps target men on testosterone replacement therapy. Both offer injection tracking, lab tracking, and Free T calculation. The differences live in depth-of-features (where TRT Plus wins) and coaching-with-cited-sources (where Calibrum wins). Below is the full breakdown so you can pick by use case, not by marketing.

60-Second Verdict

  • Pick TRT Plus if you want the deepest feature surface — four Free T calculation methods (Vermeulen plus Mazer, Södergård, Zakharov), the Testimator pharmacokinetic predictor, the E2 modulator profiles, and a research-citation knowledge base. You are comfortable in dense data interfaces and want the most powerful pure tracker available.
  • Pick Calibrum if you want a conversational AI Hormone Coach that cites Endocrine Society, AUA, and AAFP guidelines on every reply, plus an E2 symptom triage system that pattern-matches your symptoms against published profiles. You also want the Vermeulen Free T calculator in a permanently free tier with no subscription.
  • Pick both if you want TRT Plus for the pharmacokinetic depth and Calibrum for the doctor-visit prep PDF and coaching layer. Many users run both — they serve different parts of the workflow.

What Both Apps Do Well

Worth saying upfront — both apps are serious tools built for men managing legitimate TRT under physician supervision. Neither is a bodybuilding tracker, both surface educational context rather than prescribe, and both treat the user as someone who can read their own labs once they have the vocabulary.

  • Multi-medication injection tracking with site rotation visualizer (testosterone esters, anastrozole, hCG, ancillaries).
  • Lab marker tracking with reference ranges (Total T, Free T, SHBG, E2, Hematocrit, PSA, and more).
  • Vermeulen Free T calculation.
  • Lab report photo/PDF extraction.
  • Doctor-facing summary export.
  • Apple Health integration.

Where TRT Plus Clearly Wins

If we are being honest — and we are — TRT Plus has the deeper feature surface for users who want maximum analytical depth.

  • More Free T calculation methods. TRT Plus offers Vermeulen plus three other formulas (Mazer, Södergård, Zakharov). For a researcher-mindset user who wants to cross-check Free T against different equations, this is a real advantage. Calibrum offers only Vermeulen.
  • The Testimator pharmacokinetic predictor. Peak-to-trough fluctuation visualization based on your dose and frequency, now with dynamic reference ranges driven by your biomarker results. This is the most sophisticated PK modeling in the category.
  • E2 modulator profiles. Twenty-three research-backed entries with mechanisms of action (aromatization, AI properties, SHBG displacement, receptor interactions) and study citations. Calibrum does not offer this depth.
  • Responder-type classification. Whether you are a normal, low, hyper, super, or non-responder to testosterone — based on your data. Useful framing that Calibrum does not provide.
  • Bulk lab upload. Up to 15 lab images at once, parsed via OCR.
  • Pathology partner integration in AU. Direct test ordering for Australian users through their pathology partner.
  • Larger established install base. TRT Plus has been on the App Store since 2023 and has accumulated reviews and word-of-mouth in the TRT community.

Where Calibrum Wins

Calibrum's lane is coaching-on-top-of-tracking. The differences here matter most for users who have specific questions they would otherwise message a friend on Reddit or email their prescriber's nurse line for.

  • 24/7 conversational AI Hormone Coach with citations on every reply. You ask, "Is my libido drop more likely high E2 or low T at the end of the week," the Coach surfaces what the Endocrine Society guideline says, what AAFP monographs say, and the relevant E2 threshold from peer-reviewed studies. Every reply shows which source it is drawing from.
  • E2 symptom triage as a workflow. Log symptoms over time, get a triage summary that pattern-matches against published high-E2, low-E2, and unrelated profiles, weighted against your most recent E2 lab. TRT Plus surfaces AI correlations on individual data points; Calibrum builds the E2 triage as a first-class feature with its own UI.
  • Doctor Visit Prep PDF with personalized questions. Auto-generates a one- to two-page summary before each appointment with three to five specific questions to ask based on what your data shows. TRT Plus does not currently offer this.
  • Vermeulen calculator in a permanently free tier. Free T calculation is essential information. Calibrum places Vermeulen in the free tier with no subscription wall — useful for men checking whether they are even TRT candidates.
  • Clinical-TRT-only framing. The Coach is grounded in the Endocrine Society/AUA/AAFP guideline corpus, not in enhanced-athlete research. The AI does not engage with off-label cycle planning or grey-market protocol questions.
  • Tighter privacy posture. Local-only data, no account required. TRT Plus mixes local and cloud features.

Pricing in 2026

Calibrum. Weekly $4.99 or annual $49.99 (about $0.96/week) with 7-day free trial. Vermeulen Free T calculator is permanently free with no subscription.

TRT Plus. Free download with in-app purchases for premium tiers. Pricing varies by feature unlock; check the App Store for current tier costs as they have changed over time.

Both apps are competitively priced for ongoing use. If you are using the calculator only, Calibrum's free tier costs $0/year. If you are actively managing TRT with the full feature surface, both apps are well under the cost of one telehealth appointment per year.

What Neither App Does

Important boundary. Neither Calibrum nor TRT Plus — nor any other TRT app — prescribes medication, recommends dose changes, or replaces consultation with your prescribing physician. They are companion tools, useful between appointments. The decisions about starting, continuing, titrating, or stopping testosterone, anastrozole, hCG, or any other medication belong to you and your prescriber.

For severe symptoms — chest pain, severe testicular pain, signs of a blood clot, severe allergic reaction, hematocrit elevation flagged by your lab — call your prescriber or 911 immediately, regardless of what any app shows. For mental health crisis or suicidal thoughts, call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline).

One-Line Summary

Use TRT Plus if you want the deepest pure-tracker feature set with the most calculation methods. Use Calibrum if you want an AI Hormone Coach that cites clinical guidelines on every reply, plus the doctor-visit PDF workflow and the free Vermeulen calculator. Both apps can coexist on the same phone.
