VeriBag vs Entrupy — Which Handbag Authentication Tool to Use in 2026

An honest side-by-side from the team that builds VeriBag. We will not tell you VeriBag wins everywhere, because it doesn't. The right answer depends on what the bag is worth and what kind of verdict you need.

The 60-second verdict

Use VeriBag for everyday pre-purchase checks, pre-ship listing certificates on Poshmark / Depop / Vestiaire / eBay, gift-receiver private verification, and any handbag worth less than about $5,000 where you want a structured opinion in 30 seconds for under $40 a year.

Use Entrupy for insurance claims, legal disputes, estate documentation, business consignment workflows, and any handbag worth more than $5,000 where you need a written authentication certificate with a financial guarantee behind the verdict.

For bags between $2,000 and $5,000 in active resale, many sellers run both — VeriBag for the pre-ship QR link in the listing, Entrupy for the certificate the high-end buyer asks for after the offer.

When VeriBag is the right call

You're about to bid on a $1,650 Chanel Classic Flap on Vestiaire. The seller posted six photos. You want to compare those photos against documented Chanel manufacturing details before you commit. Entrupy is not available because you don't have the bag in hand. VeriBag lets you upload existing listing photos and runs the 22-point check against them.

You list 30 bags a month on Poshmark and Depop and lose money on disputes. Every dispute freezes funds for two to four weeks. VeriBag's QR verify link pasted into the listing description gives every buyer a 22-point breakdown before they buy — disputes drop from "I think it's fake" to specific checkpoint discussions, and discussions are not freezes. Entrupy's $99 per scan would consume your margin on most listings.

You received a Louis Vuitton as a gift and you want to know privately. Walking it into an authenticator feels awkward. VeriBag runs on your phone, in private, and no one sees the result but you.

You're at an estate sale with 90 seconds to decide whether $400 for a Kelly is a steal or a fake. Entrupy isn't there. The 22-point check is.

When Entrupy is the right call

You're about to wire $8,400 for a vintage Hermès Birkin from a private seller. The bag is in hand and the price is high enough that "consistent on 22 visible checkpoints" is not a strong enough verdict to write the wire. Entrupy's microscopy goes below what your iPhone camera can see and the certificate matters if the deal goes sideways.

You're filing an insurance claim on a $12,000 lost Chanel. The insurer wants a written authentication certificate from a recognized service. VeriBag's PDF report can be supplementary; Entrupy's certificate is the primary document.

You run a consignment business and your buyers expect Entrupy verification at retail. Workflow integration, certificate registry, and brand recognition outweigh per-scan cost at business volume.

You're settling an estate with a luxury handbag collection. Probate-defensible authentication needs a written certificate. The 22-point reference check is not enough.

The pricing math

VeriBag's annual plan is $39.99 — about 40 cents per bag if you check 100 bags a year, which is normal volume for an active reseller. Entrupy's consumer scan is approximately $99 per bag. The breakeven is roughly one bag per year: if you check more than one bag, VeriBag's annual plan pays for itself versus a single Entrupy scan. The Lifetime tier at $149.99 pays back in about one and a half Entrupy scans and runs forever after.

The financial logic only flips when the bag's value justifies the $99 cost of a certificate — typically above $5,000, or any transaction where insurance, dispute defense, or estate documentation matters.

What about running both?

For bags in the $2,000–5,000 range that you're reselling on Vestiaire, Fashionphile, or to private buyers, the most common professional workflow is:

  1. Source check with VeriBag — before you buy at the estate sale, consignment shop, or private message. 30-second decision. $0 marginal cost on Lifetime plan.
  2. Pre-ship VeriBag QR link — paste into the listing description on Poshmark / Depop / Vestiaire. Reduces dispute risk before money changes hands.
  3. Entrupy certificate for the high-end buyer — when a buyer offers a price point that justifies the $99 cost (typically $3,000+), pay for an Entrupy scan and pass the certificate to the buyer. The fee gets recovered in the closing margin.

This is the workflow we hear from resellers running Hermès Kelly and Birkin volume. VeriBag handles the everyday velocity; Entrupy handles the trophies.

Verdict

VeriBag and Entrupy solve different problems. VeriBag is a high-volume, low-friction reference checklist for everyday pre-purchase, pre-ship, and private verification. Entrupy is a low-volume, high-stakes authentication service with a certificate the world recognizes. The right tool depends on the verdict's job: is it a fast sanity check or a document that has to hold up?

If you're new to secondhand luxury authentication, the most common path is: start with VeriBag for one bag, see whether the workflow fits your real activity (buying, selling, gifting), and decide from there whether you ever need the Entrupy certificate layer.
