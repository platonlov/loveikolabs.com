Folik vs Regrow Hair AI

An honest head-to-head of two hair loss companion apps. We make Folik and disclose that here.

Both apps target the same audience: adults with pattern hair loss who want to track Norwood/Ludwig stage, log treatments, and monitor progress photos. The differences live in source citations (where Folik wins), installed base and maturity (where Regrow wins), and the free-tier policy on the Norwood calculator (where Folik wins). Below is the full breakdown.

60-Second Verdict

  • Pick Regrow Hair AI if you want the most established hair loss app with a large user base, a polished mature UX, and a product library spanning treatments and reviews. It is the category default for a reason — the team has been iterating since 2024 and the App Store reviews reflect it.
  • Pick Folik if you want every AI Coach reply to cite a specific source — AAD guideline section, ISHRS resource, FDA prescribing information, or named PubMed study with year and authors. You also want the Norwood calculator in a permanently free tier with no signup, and a photo journal that uses ghost overlay alignment for genuinely repeatable framing.
  • Pick both if you want Regrow's mature UX and product library alongside Folik's cited-sources Coach. They serve different parts of the workflow and many users run both.

What Both Apps Do Well

Worth stating upfront — both apps are serious tools for adults managing pattern hair loss. Neither is a supplement marketing funnel or transplant clinic referral service. Both treat the user as an informed adult who can handle nuanced clinical information.

  • AI-powered Norwood (men) / Ludwig (women) staging from photos.
  • Photo progress tracker with side-by-side comparison.
  • Treatment reminders for minoxidil, finasteride, supplements, and custom routines.
  • Growth insights and trend visualization over time.
  • Educational content on common pattern hair loss treatments.
  • iPhone native, Apple Health integration for relevant data.
  • Privacy posture appropriate for medical-adjacent data.

Where Regrow Hair AI Clearly Wins

If we are being honest — and we are — Regrow Hair AI has the longer track record and the larger user base.

  • Established #1 position. Regrow self-describes as "the #1 hair loss app" and the App Store reviews suggest the team has earned that with consistent iteration since 2024.
  • Product library with treatment reviews. Browse clinically-backed treatment paths inside the app — Folik does not have an in-app product library.
  • Mature onboarding flow. The user experience benefits from two years of iteration. New users get oriented faster.
  • Larger community signal. More reviews, more word-of-mouth in hair loss subreddits and forums.
  • Polished growth insights. The trend charts and growth visualizations are mature.

Where Folik Wins

Folik's lane is the evidence-citation depth and the honest off-label disclosure.

  • Every Coach reply cites a specific source. Ask "how long until finasteride works", the Coach replies with the Kaufman et al. 1998 trial timeline plus the AAD guideline section. Ask "is dutasteride approved for hair loss in the US", the Coach replies "No — FDA-approved for BPH only in the US; approved for hair loss in South Korea and Japan" with the FDA prescribing information reference. This citation density is the main differentiator.
  • Free Norwood/Ludwig calculator with no signup. Open the app, take a photo, get a stage in 30 seconds. No account, no email, no paywall. Most competitor apps lock the staging behind a subscription wall or at least require account creation; Folik does not.
  • Ghost overlay alignment in the photo journal. Repeatable framing is the single most important variable in trustworthy progress tracking — most apparent "progress" or "regression" between non-calibrated photos is just lighting or angle change. Folik's previous-scan-ghosts-into-viewfinder approach is the most reliable solution we have seen in this category.
  • Pixel-density measurement on calibrated regions. Quantitative, not just visual.
  • Explicit off-label disclosure. Dutasteride for hair loss is off-label in the US. Oral minoxidil for hair loss is off-label. Folik surfaces these explicitly with the FDA prescribing context. Many competitor apps treat these as standard treatments without disclosing the regulatory status.
  • Honest PFS framing. Post-finasteride syndrome is a contested clinical area. Folik does not dismiss it nor catastrophize it — the Coach surfaces the published evidence, the FDA-required label warnings, and recommends baseline sexual function documentation before starting.
  • Explicit FDA Pregnancy Category X warnings. Finasteride and dutasteride pose fetal risk and are inappropriate for women of childbearing potential. Folik surfaces this prominently every time these medications come up in a women's-hair-loss context.
  • Local-only photo storage. Scalp photos are sensitive. Folik keeps them on your device.

What Neither App Does

Important boundary. Neither Folik nor Regrow Hair AI — nor any other hair loss app — prescribes medication, diagnoses your hair loss type, or replaces a dermatologist. They are companion tracking tools. The decisions about starting, continuing, titrating, or stopping finasteride, dutasteride, topical minoxidil, oral minoxidil, microneedling, or any treatment belong to you and your dermatologist.

Hair loss with skin changes (redness, scarring, itching, burning, scaling), patchy bald spots with smooth skin, sudden severe shedding, or accompanying symptoms (significant weight changes, thyroid symptoms, severe stress, postpartum) warrants dermatology evaluation — not just app tracking. For sexual side effects on finasteride or dutasteride, contact your prescriber immediately. For severe symptoms or signs of allergic reaction, call 911.
