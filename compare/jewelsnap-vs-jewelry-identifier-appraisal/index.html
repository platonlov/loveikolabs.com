Where Jewelry Identifier — Appraisal is stronger

Larger user base and review volume

Ideas All Day launched their app in 2024 and has accumulated 231 ratings versus our 63. More reviews generally mean the app has been tested across a wider variety of use cases. If you weigh "stress-tested by lots of users" heavily, this is a real point in their favor.

Lower minimum iOS version

Their app works on iOS 14.0+. JewelSnap requires iOS 17.0+. If you're using an older iPhone (6s, 7, 8, SE 1st gen) that can't update to iOS 17, JewelSnap won't install. Their app will.

More languages out of the box

Jewelry Identifier — Appraisal supports more interface languages today. We're rolling out localization over the next quarters but lag here as of May 2026.

Where JewelSnap is stronger

Era dating across nine historical periods

This is the single biggest differentiator. Jewelry Identifier — Appraisal tells you what metal and stones a piece contains. JewelSnap also tells you whether it's Georgian, Victorian, Edwardian, Art Nouveau, Art Deco, Retro, Mid-Century, or Contemporary — which often doubles the appraised value over raw-material valuation alone.

An Art Deco diamond ring valued by materials alone might come in at $3,000. With era pedigree confirmed, the same ring can appraise at $8,000-15,000. If you're sorting inheritance, doing estate sales, or buying vintage, this matters.

Hallmark coverage for 50+ countries

Jewelry Identifier — Appraisal decodes hallmarks for US, UK, France, and Germany — covering maybe 70% of jewelry circulating in North American markets. JewelSnap decodes hallmarks from 50+ countries including Russia, Eastern Europe, Middle East, India, China, Japan, Thailand, and major South American markets.

If your grandmother emigrated from a non-Western country, or you buy at international auctions, the hallmark coverage gap is significant.

Interpol-backed stolen property check

This feature is unique to JewelSnap. Cross-references scanned jewelry against the Interpol Stolen Works of Art Database before purchase. Particularly useful for estate sales, online marketplaces, and private-party sales where provenance is uncertain.

Higher rating from a smaller base

4.81 stars vs 4.69 stars. JewelSnap users report slightly higher satisfaction. The gap is small but consistent across recent reviews. Worth noting: smaller review counts have higher variance, so this gap may narrow as our review count grows.

PDF appraisal reports

JewelSnap exports professional-format PDF reports suitable for insurance documentation, estate division, and resale listings (eBay, Poshmark). Jewelry Identifier — Appraisal does not currently offer PDF export.

Annual subscription cost

$29.99/year for JewelSnap Pro versus approximately $39.99/year for Jewelry Identifier — Appraisal. Small absolute difference but a clear win on annual pricing.

Which one to choose

Choose JewelSnap if you:

  • Are sorting inherited jewelry and need to date pieces by era
  • Have jewelry with non-US/EU hallmarks (Russian, Middle Eastern, Asian, Eastern European, South American)
  • Buy at estate sales or online marketplaces and want stolen-property verification
  • Resell on eBay/Poshmark/Mercari and need PDF appraisal reports for listings
  • Have an iPhone running iOS 17+ (most iPhones from 2018 onward)
  • Want the lowest annual subscription cost

Choose Jewelry Identifier — Appraisal if you:

  • Use an older iPhone that can't run iOS 17
  • Strongly prefer apps with larger review bases (more "social proof")
  • Only need basic material and value information, not era or international hallmarks
  • Want a non-English interface language they support

Try them both

Both apps offer free trials. The honest recommendation: download both, scan the same three pieces of jewelry with each, and see which results feel more accurate and complete for your specific collection. You'll pay nothing in the trial periods, and you'll have an informed answer in fifteen minutes.
