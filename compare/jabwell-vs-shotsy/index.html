JabWell vs Shotsy

Honest comparison of two iOS GLP-1 tracker apps in 2026.

Disclosure: we (Loveiko Labs) make JabWell. We are not affiliated with Shotsy. This comparison is honest about both apps — Shotsy is excellent at what it does, and we say so where it wins.

The 60-second verdict

Pick Shotsy if you want a polished, mature, pure GLP-1 injection tracker with the largest install base and review count. It has been on the App Store since 2024, supports every major brand and oral GLP-1, syncs cleanly with Apple Health, and exports a clean doctor-visit PDF. If your job is "log my shot, remind me, show me a chart," Shotsy nails it.

Pick JabWell if you want a tracker plus a coaching layer — specifically a 24/7 AI nurse chat with source citations on every reply (FDA, AAFP, Mayo Clinic, Cleveland Clinic), a protein coach calibrated for the GLP-1 appetite curve, a plateau detector that turns four-week stalls into specific prescriber questions, and a weekly hair-and-skin photo journal. If your job is "help me through the side effects and the plateau, not just log them," JabWell is built for that.

Pick both if you are 18 months in, have a system that works in Shotsy, and want JabWell only during titration windows or when a side effect kicks in. They do not conflict — different jobs, different tools.

Where Shotsy clearly wins

We do not pretend our app beats Shotsy at every category. Here are the honest points where Shotsy is the better choice:

  1. Maturity and install base. Shotsy has been on the App Store since 2024, has thousands of user reviews, and has shipped consistent updates including 2026 additions like Foundayo support and body composition metrics (lean body mass, body fat, waist circumference). JabWell is newer and has fewer reviews — that is a fact, not an opinion.
  2. Most detailed medication-level chart. Shotsy's estimated medication-level visualization is the most-praised feature in user reviews, with the cleanest curves we have seen across all GLP-1 trackers.
  3. Body composition tracking. Shotsy added lean body mass, body fat, waist circumference, and blood glucose tracking in 2026. JabWell tracks weight only in version 1.0; body composition is on our roadmap.
  4. Foundayo (orforglipron) support. Shotsy added Foundayo support when it launched. JabWell currently supports Foundayo only as a custom medication without the medication-level chart.
  5. Nurse-like documentation polish. One of Shotsy's most-cited reviews comes from a nurse who praises the app's "documentation sense" — the clinical-data-trail feel of the dose, weight, and side-effect log. That polish is real and earned over multiple update cycles.
  6. Reminder permission flow. Shotsy's 2026 update specifically improved reminder permission prompts. Small UX detail, but the kind of thing a long-running app gets right.

If those six things are what you need from a GLP-1 app, Shotsy is the right pick. Honestly.

Where JabWell wins

And here is where JabWell does something Shotsy does not:

  1. AI nurse chat with source citations on every reply. Ask "is sulfur burping normal on Mounjaro?" and the reply cites FDA prescribing info, AAFP guidelines, and Mayo Clinic patient material. Ask "how long does nausea last on Wegovy?" and the reply pulls the specific FDA clinical-trial timeline. Shotsy is a tracker — it does not have a chat layer.
  2. Protein coach calibrated for GLP-1 appetite. JabWell calculates a personalized daily protein target (around 1g/kg) and suggests meals tuned for the GLP-1 appetite curve — small portions, high density, easy on a stomach with delayed gastric emptying. Shotsy lets you log protein but does not coach.
  3. Plateau detector that generates prescriber questions. When your weight stays in a ±0.5kg band for four-plus weeks, JabWell generates a structured set of titration-timing questions to bring to your prescriber, sourced from FDA prescribing information for your specific medication. Shotsy shows you the chart; JabWell turns the chart into a conversation.
  4. Weekly hair-and-skin photo journal. GLP-1 shedding is one of the most-discussed side effects on Reddit and TikTok. JabWell prompts a weekly journal so you can compare against photos, not memory. Optional AI analysis flags changes week-over-week. Shotsy does not have a photo journal.
  5. Three-tier side-effect triage. Every side-effect log entry produces a verdict — common / worth flagging / stop and call prescriber. The triage logic is grounded in FDA prescribing information for the medication you are on. Shotsy logs side effects but does not triage them.

When neither app is enough

Neither JabWell nor Shotsy replaces:

  • Your prescriber. Endocrinologist, primary care, obesity-medicine specialist, or telehealth provider. Dose decisions, prior authorization, and clinical monitoring belong to them.
  • Pharmacy verification. Compounded GLP-1 quality varies; verify your pharmacy is a licensed 503A or 503B facility.
  • Emergency care. For vomiting more than 24 hours, severe abdominal pain, signs of pancreatitis or allergic reaction — call 911 or Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222 in the US. Neither app is built for emergencies.
  • Mental health support. GLP-1s have FDA-noted concerns about suicidal ideation in some users. If you experience severe depression or intrusive thoughts, contact your prescriber or the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline immediately.

The one-sentence decision

Use Shotsy if you mainly want to track. Use JabWell if you mainly want to learn and decide.
