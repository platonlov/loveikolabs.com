Overview

How we ranked these apps

2026 is a turning point for eczema apps: EczemaWise, the National Eczema Association's long-running tracker, stopped accepting new accounts and will close to existing users on December 18, 2026. If you need a new eczema tracker — or your first one — these are the six apps worth looking at.

Across 6 apps we evaluated the features that matter to an actual user, not the ones that look good on a landing page:

  • Flare tracking. Dates, severity and progression.
  • Trigger logging. Food, stress, weather, allergens, products and routines — and how the app helps you compare them with flares.
  • Severity measurement. A structured score (such as a POEM-style measure) rather than vague impressions.
  • Photo timeline. Dated photos, and where they are stored.
  • Doctor usefulness. A summary a dermatologist or pediatrician can read quickly.
  • Privacy and longevity. Account requirements, analytics, and whether the app will still be around.

None of these apps diagnose eczema or replace a dermatologist, allergist or pediatrician. We say this once and assume it for the rest.

02

Skintrics — Best for Multiple Skin Conditions + Food

What it is. A skin tracker for eczema, psoriasis, acne, rosacea and topical steroid withdrawal with a 0–10 daily score, photos, trigger correlations rated by confidence, and a food and gut-health scoring feature.

Best for. People managing more than one skin condition or suspecting diet-related flares.

Strengths. Confidence-rated triggers with mechanism explanations; food scoring.

Limitations. 4.1★ from 7 ratings. Premium required after a 7-day trial.

03

EczemaLess — Best AI Severity Rating from Photos

What it is. An AI app for diagnosed atopic dermatitis patients that suggests a severity rating from a skin image and tracks triggers, treatments and care plans. It classifies itself as clinical decision support intended for licensed professionals.

Best for. Patients whose clinician wants photo-based severity tracking.

Strengths. 4.6★ from 66 ratings; around since 2019.

Limitations. Explicitly not intended for self-diagnosis or self-treatment by patients.

04

Bearable — Best General Symptom Tracker

What it is. A flexible symptom, mood and habit tracker that finds correlations with sleep, food, weather, stress and medication.

Best for. People tracking eczema alongside other conditions.

Strengths. 4.8★ from 6K+ ratings; very flexible; exports.

Limitations. Not eczema-specific — no severity measure or photo timeline built for skin.

05

Aysa — Best for "What Is This Rash?" Research

What it is. A skin symptom checker from VisualDx: take a photo and get symptom matches with educational content, with an image library representing all skin types.

Best for. Preparing questions about an unfamiliar rash before seeing a clinician.

Strengths. 4.6★ from 390+ ratings; strong skin-of-color image library; images discarded after analysis.

Limitations. A symptom checker, not a long-term eczema tracker.

EczemaWise — Legacy Pick (Closing December 2026)

What none of these apps do

None of these apps — including ours — diagnose eczema, identify infections, or prescribe topical steroids, calcineurin inhibitors, biologics or JAK inhibitors. Those decisions belong to a dermatologist or other clinician.

Painful clustered blisters or punched-out sores in eczema can signal eczema herpeticum and need same-day medical care. Spreading redness, warmth, pus or fever can mean infection. Facial or throat swelling or trouble breathing needs 911 or your local emergency number.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why did you rank your own app first?

Salvora combines an eczema-specific trigger and care-routine log, a POEM-style severity trend, an on-device photo timeline and a doctor-ready summary, with no account or third-party analytics. It is new and has few ratings; Bearable and Aysa have much longer track records for their narrower jobs.

What is the best replacement for EczemaWise?

Look for flare and trigger logging, care-routine history and an appointment summary — the core of what EczemaWise did. Salvora is built around those jobs. Export or screenshot what you can from EczemaWise before December 18, 2026.

Is there a free eczema tracker?

Bearable offers free general symptom tracking. Most eczema-specific trackers, including Salvora and Skintrics, use a free trial and then a subscription.

Can an app tell me what's triggering my eczema?

An app can help you see patterns across weeks of logs. It cannot confirm allergies — suspected food or contact allergies should be assessed by an allergist or dermatologist.

Which eczema app is best for babies and kids?

Salvora supports parents organizing a child's history — flares, sleep, foods, routines and photos — for the pediatrician. Babies with severe or infected-looking eczema need prompt medical review.
