Best Fatty Liver Apps 2026

Six iPhone apps for people with fatty liver (MASLD, formerly NAFLD) — ranked honestly, including our own with full disclosure.

See the ranking

Overview

How we ranked these apps

Fatty liver apps barely existed two years ago; now there are a dozen, almost all with small review counts. Most focus on food scoring. Very few help with the other half of the problem — understanding the blood tests that started it all. This ranking covers six apps worth trying and who each is for.

Across 6 apps we evaluated the features that matter to an actual user, not the ones that look good on a landing page:

  • Lab understanding. Can the app read your report and explain ALT, AST, GGT and platelets against your own reference ranges? Does it calculate FIB-4?
  • Meal guidance. Photo scoring, nutrient budgets, barcode scanning — and whether the advice is practical.
  • Guidance quality. Is there an AI or expert guide, and does it show sources?
  • Doctor usefulness. Can you export a summary for your GP or hepatologist?
  • Stage coverage. Early fatty liver vs advanced disease (cirrhosis, ascites) needs different tracking.
  • Privacy. Account requirements and where your records live.

None of these apps diagnose liver disease or replace a doctor or hepatologist. We say this once and assume it for the rest.

The Ranking

6 best fatty liver apps for iPhone in 2026

01

Hepatica — Best for Understanding Your Liver Blood Tests (Our App, Full Disclosure)

Disclosure. Hepatica is built by Loveiko Labs — the studio publishing this article. We rank it first for people who want their lab results explained and a doctor-ready summary; for detailed daily nutrient budgets, see LiverPal and Liverly.

What it is. Reads lab reports by photo, PDF or manual entry and explains each value against your report's reference range; calculates FIB-4 when values are present; meal photo check with one practical swap; AI Liver Guide with visible sources; evening check-ins, alcohol-free days, doctor summary PDF.

Best for. Newly diagnosed people, anyone tracking liver enzymes between tests, GLP-1 users, people rebuilding after alcohol.

Strengths. Only app here combining report reading, FIB-4 and a sourced AI guide. No account; records on device; no ads. 20 languages.

Limitations. Very new (May 2026) with a single rating so far. Does not set sodium or protein budgets for advanced liver disease.

02

LiverPal — Best Clinical Nutrition Tracker (incl. Cirrhosis & Ascites)

What it is. A clinical nutrition tracker for fatty liver, cirrhosis and ascites that tracks sodium, added sugars, saturated fat and protein, with an AI meal scanner and liver-specific food grading.

Best for. People whose clinician gave them nutrient targets — especially sodium and protein in advanced disease.

Strengths. 4.9★ from 11 ratings. Condition-specific targets, photo meal scanning.

Limitations. Nutrition-first; no lab report explanation or FIB-4.

LiverPal on the App Store

03

Liverly — Best Plate Score

What it is. Photograph any meal and get a 0–100 liver score computed against daily budgets personalized to your diagnosis, from MASLD and MASH to fibrosis and cirrhosis.

Best for. People who want a simple number for every meal.

Strengths. Tracks added sugar, saturated fat and calories against personalized budgets.

Limitations. Launched July 2026 — 4.2★ from 6 ratings. Food-only.

Liverly: Fatty Liver Diet on the App Store

04

LiverAlertMe — Best Specialist-Designed Plan

What it is. A fatty liver tracker developed by a liver specialist, with a personalized action plan, traffic-light meal suggestions from text or voice, and weight-loss pacing guidance.

Best for. People who want a structured plan from a clinical designer.

Strengths. Specialist-designed; warns against too-rapid weight loss.

Limitations. 4.0★ from 19 ratings. No photo lab reading.

LiverAlertMe: Fatty Liver Care on the App Store

05

MyFiltr — Best Grocery Barcode Scanner

What it is. Scan food barcodes to flag liver-unfriendly ingredients, log a daily Liver Load score, scan lab reports for trends and generate clinician-ready reports.

Best for. Shoppers who want quick ingredient clarity in the store.

Strengths. Barcode scanning plus lab trends and clinician reports.

Limitations. Very small review base (2 ratings). No FIB-4.

MyFiltr on the App Store

06

DietSensor Nash — Best for Learning the Basics

What it is. A NAFLD/NASH nutrition app with an academy of lessons, diet guidance and an AI-based dietitian.

Best for. People who want education and structured diet recommendations.

Strengths. 4.3★ from 23 ratings; around since 2023 — the longest track record here.

Limitations. Uses older NAFLD/NASH framing. No lab report reading.

What none of these apps do

None of these apps — including ours — diagnose fatty liver, fibrosis or cirrhosis, or replace a doctor, hepatologist or dietitian. FIB-4 is a screening index, not a diagnosis; elastography, imaging or other tests may be needed.

Stopping heavy drinking can require medical support. Vomiting blood, yellow skin or eyes, confusion or severe abdominal pain need emergency care — call 911 or your local emergency number.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why did you rank your own app first?

Hepatica is the only app here that reads your lab report, explains each value against your own reference range and calculates FIB-4 — the part of a fatty liver diagnosis most people understand least. It is also the newest with the fewest ratings; LiverPal and Liverly are stronger for detailed nutrient budgets.

What is the best app for fatty liver diet?

For daily nutrient budgets, LiverPal or Liverly. For a quick check of a meal with one realistic swap, Hepatica. For grocery shopping, MyFiltr.

Is there an app that calculates FIB-4?

Yes — Hepatica calculates FIB-4 when age, AST, ALT and platelets are available. Several standalone medical calculators also compute it.

Can an app reverse fatty liver?

No app reverses anything. Habits do — gradual weight loss, better diet quality, activity and less alcohol, as discussed with your clinician. Apps help you keep those habits and see whether your blood tests move.

Do these apps work for cirrhosis?

LiverPal and Liverly advertise diagnosis-specific targets including cirrhosis. Advanced liver disease needs close medical care; use any app only alongside your hepatology team.
