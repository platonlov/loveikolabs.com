Best GLP-1 Tracker Apps in 2026

Honest 7-app ranking for Ozempic, Mounjaro, Wegovy, Zepbound, Rybelsus, Saxenda, Victoza, Trulicity, and compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide.

Disclosure up front: Loveiko Labs makes one of these apps (JabWell). It is placed at #2 with reasons. We tried to keep this honest because being caught lying about competitors in 2026 — when every prospective buyer cross-checks one tap away — is worse than missing a download.

How we ranked

Five criteria, each weighted equally:

  1. Tracking depth. Dose log, site rotation, medication-level chart, side-effect log, Apple Health sync.
  2. Coaching layer. AI chat, protein coach, plateau detection, side-effect triage, doctor-visit prep.
  3. Medication coverage. Brand-name GLP-1s plus compounded, plus oral options like Rybelsus and Foundayo.
  4. Privacy and pricing. Local-first storage, no health-data sale, fair subscription.
  5. Maturity. Review base, update cadence, stability across iOS versions.

Apps that are pure marketing pages with thin tracking (Gala, ShotTrack) and apps that have not been updated in 18+ months were excluded.

1. Shotsy — Best pure GLP-1 tracker

Available: iOS, Android. App Store ID 6499510249.

Shotsy has been the reference GLP-1 tracker on iOS since 2024. It supports every major brand (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound, Rybelsus, Saxenda, Victoza, Trulicity) plus compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and added Foundayo (orforglipron) support in 2026. The estimated medication-level chart is the most-praised feature across community reviews — clean curves based on peer-reviewed clinical data. Apple Health sync pulls weight, calories, protein, and water automatically. The doctor-visit PDF is clean and clinical.

2026 updates added body fat, lean body mass, waist circumference, and blood glucose tracking — useful for users who want to monitor more than weight alone.

Where Shotsy wins: install base, review depth, polish, medication-level chart accuracy, body composition tracking.

Where Shotsy is limited: no AI chat layer; no plateau detector that generates prescriber questions; no hair-and-skin photo journal; no side-effect triage beyond logging.

If your job is "log my shot, see my curve, export to my doctor" — Shotsy is the best choice on iOS in 2026.

2. JabWell — Best coaching layer (this is our app)

Available: iOS. App Store ID 6764151811.

Disclosure: Loveiko Labs (publisher of this page) makes JabWell.

JabWell is positioned for users who want tracking plus a coaching layer. The 24/7 AI nurse chat cites FDA prescribing information, AAFP guidelines, Mayo Clinic, and Cleveland Clinic on every reply — so when you ask "is sulfur burping normal on Mounjaro?", you get a source-cited answer plus the threshold for when to call your prescriber. The protein coach is calibrated for GLP-1 appetite suppression (~1g/kg targets with meal suggestions that survive a slow-emptying stomach). The plateau detector flags four-week stalls and generates structured prescriber questions. The weekly hair-and-skin photo journal addresses one of the most-discussed GLP-1 side effects (shedding from rapid weight loss).

Where JabWell wins: AI nurse with source citations, plateau detector with prescriber questions, hair journal, protein coach, three-tier side-effect triage.

Where JabWell is behind Shotsy: install base (newer app), no body composition tracking yet, Foundayo only as custom logging.

If your job is "help me through the side effects and the plateau, not just log them" — this is the app we built for that.

3. GLP AI — Best dedicated AI coach alternative

Available: iOS. App Store ID 6755669956.

GLP AI launched in late 2025 and quickly built a passionate user base. It combines injection tracking with an AI coach and an AI nutrition scanner (snap a meal photo, get protein/fiber/calorie breakdown tailored for GLP-1 users). The Journey Card feature combines photos, weight logs, and milestones into a shareable timeline. User reviews specifically praise the cleaner interface and food logging.

Where GLP AI wins: AI nutrition scanner, polished UX, active community.

Where it is limited: AI chat does not surface explicit source citations on every reply; no plateau detector with structured prescriber questions; no hair journal.

The closest direct competitor to JabWell on feature philosophy. If JabWell isn't a fit, GLP AI is the most likely next stop.

4. Pep — Best multi-language GLP-1 tracker

Available: iOS, Android.

Pep supports 10 languages — English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, and Traditional Chinese. The "GLP-1 body-level indicator" is its differentiating visual — a clean representation of when medication is most active in your system. Multi-medication support including Rybelsus oral and compounded GLP-1s.

Where Pep wins: language coverage, body-level visualization, polished cross-platform availability.

Where it is limited: coaching layer is lighter than JabWell or GLP AI; AI features are basic.

If you are outside the US and want a GLP-1 tracker in your native language, Pep is the strongest choice.

5. Glapp — Best for understanding the weekly GLP-1 cycle

Available: iOS, web (glapp.io).

Glapp's distinguishing concept is "phases" — it breaks the weekly GLP-1 cycle into distinct stages and predicts when hunger, cravings, and side effects will show up. The free tier is genuinely useful, and the premium AI question feature answers GLP-1 questions backed by research and real-world user data.

Where Glapp wins: phase-based understanding of the weekly cycle, generous free tier, AI question feature.

Where it is limited: primary focus on Mounjaro/Zepbound; less polished for Saxenda/Victoza/Trulicity users; no doctor-visit PDF.

6. GLP-1.COM — Best dashboard view

Available: iOS. App Store ID 6749719804. Developer: Kyral Labs, Inc.

GLP-1.COM's hook is the dashboard — daily protein, fiber, water, activity, current medication levels, upcoming shots, and side effects all on one screen. The AI GLP-1 Coach has been improved through 2025-2026. Supports the major brands plus generic compounded.

Where GLP-1.COM wins: single-screen dashboard density, improving AI coach.

Where it is limited: newer, smaller review base; no hair journal; AI coach less source-cited than JabWell's.

7. Semaglutide App for GLP-1 — Best for prior authorization fights

Available: iOS. App Store ID 6451262352.

This app's unusual feature is a Prior Authorization (PA) help tool — checklists, coverage guides, and PDF templates for Zepbound, Ozempic, Mounjaro, and Wegovy insurance fights. If you are still arguing with your insurance company over GLP-1 coverage, this is the only app that explicitly addresses that workflow. Also includes protein and hydration tracking, weight log, and an AI calorie goal calculator.

Where Semaglutide App wins: prior authorization workflow — unique to this app.

Where it is limited: tracking polish is behind Shotsy; no AI nurse chat layer; no hair journal.

What none of these apps replace

  • Your prescriber. Dose decisions, prior authorization sign-off, and clinical monitoring belong to your endocrinologist, primary care provider, obesity-medicine specialist, or telehealth prescriber.
  • Emergency care. Severe abdominal pain, signs of pancreatitis, allergic reaction, severe dehydration, vision changes, or severe depression — call 911 or Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222 (US). None of these apps are equipped for emergencies.
  • Pharmacy verification. Compounded GLP-1 quality varies. Verify your pharmacy is a licensed 503A or 503B facility before injecting anything.
