Before we start

Disclosure up front

Loveiko Labs makes one of these apps (JabWell). It is placed at #1 with reasons. We explain why it is first and say plainly where another app is the better pick.

Five criteria, each weighted equally:

  1. Tracking depth. Dose log, site rotation, medication-level chart, side-effect log, Apple Health sync.
  2. Coaching layer. AI chat, protein coach, plateau detection, side-effect triage, doctor-visit prep.
  3. Medication coverage. Brand-name GLP-1s plus compounded, plus oral options like Rybelsus and Foundayo.
  4. Privacy and pricing. Local-first storage, no health-data sale, fair subscription.
  5. Maturity. Review base, update cadence, stability across iOS versions.

Apps with very limited tracking and apps not updated in 18+ months were excluded.

02

Shotsy — Best pure GLP-1 tracker

Available: iOS, Android. App Store ID 6499510249.

Shotsy has been the reference GLP-1 tracker on iOS since 2024. It supports every major brand (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound, Rybelsus, Saxenda, Victoza, Trulicity) plus compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and added Foundayo (orforglipron) support in 2026. The estimated medication-level chart is the most-praised feature across community reviews — clean curves based on peer-reviewed clinical data. Apple Health sync pulls weight, calories, protein, and water automatically. The doctor-visit PDF is clean and clinical.

2026 updates added body fat, lean body mass, waist circumference, and blood glucose tracking — useful for users who want to monitor more than weight alone.

Where Shotsy wins: install base, review depth, polish, medication-level chart accuracy, body composition tracking.

Where Shotsy is limited: no AI chat layer; no plateau detector that generates prescriber questions; no hair-and-skin photo journal; no side-effect triage beyond logging.

If your job is "log my shot, see my curve, export to my doctor" — Shotsy is the best choice on iOS in 2026.

GLP AI — Best dedicated AI coach alternative

Available: iOS. App Store ID 6755669956.

GLP AI launched in late 2025 and quickly built a passionate user base. It combines injection tracking with an AI coach and an AI nutrition scanner (snap a meal photo, get protein/fiber/calorie breakdown tailored for GLP-1 users). The Journey Card feature combines photos, weight logs, and milestones into a shareable timeline. User reviews specifically praise the cleaner interface and food logging.

Where GLP AI wins: AI nutrition scanner, polished UX, active community.

Where it is limited: AI chat does not surface explicit source citations on every reply; no plateau detector with structured prescriber questions; no hair journal.

The closest direct competitor to JabWell on feature philosophy. If JabWell isn't a fit, GLP AI is the most likely next stop.

Pep — Best multi-language GLP-1 tracker

Available: iOS, Android.

Pep supports 10 languages — English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, and Traditional Chinese. The "GLP-1 body-level indicator" is its differentiating visual — a clean representation of when medication is most active in your system. Multi-medication support including Rybelsus oral and compounded GLP-1s.

Where Pep wins: language coverage, body-level visualization, polished cross-platform availability.

Where it is limited: coaching layer is lighter than JabWell or GLP AI; AI features are basic.

If you are outside the US and want a GLP-1 tracker in your native language, Pep is the strongest choice.

What none of these apps replace

  • Your prescriber. Dose decisions, prior authorization sign-off, and clinical monitoring belong to your endocrinologist, primary care provider, obesity-medicine specialist, or telehealth prescriber.
  • Emergency care. Severe abdominal pain, signs of pancreatitis, allergic reaction, severe dehydration, vision changes, or severe depression — call 911 or your local emergency number; if you have thoughts of harming yourself, call or text 988 (US). None of these apps are equipped for emergencies.
  • Pharmacy verification. Compounded GLP-1 quality varies. Verify your pharmacy is a licensed 503A or 503B facility before injecting anything.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why is JabWell ranked #1 if you make it?

We ranked on five equally-weighted criteria: tracking depth, coaching layer, medication coverage, privacy and pricing, and maturity. JabWell scores highest on the coaching layer — source-cited AI nurse, plateau detector, hair journal — and is close to Shotsy on tracking depth and medication coverage (Shotsy adds body composition and native Foundayo support). Shotsy leads on install base and body composition. On our rubric, JabWell edges out Shotsy by the coaching dimension. Where Shotsy is the better fit, we say so in its entry.

Do any of these apps replace my prescriber?

No. Every app on this list is a tracking and coaching tool — not a prescribing, diagnosing, or monitoring service. Dose changes, clinical monitoring (kidney function, thyroid markers, pancreatitis screening), and prior authorization decisions belong entirely to your prescriber.

Which apps support compounded semaglutide?

JabWell, Shotsy, GLP AI, Pep, and Glapp all support compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide as first-class tracking options. GLP-1.COM supports generic compounded. Semaglutide App includes it in PA documentation templates. If your pharmacy is a 503A or 503B facility, any of these apps will handle your log correctly — the medications are entered manually.

Is there an app that works outside the US?

Pep is the strongest option outside the US — it supports 10 languages including Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, and Traditional Chinese and is available on both iOS and Android. JabWell and Shotsy are English-only but are available in 168 countries on the App Store.

Are these apps private — do they sell health data?

None of the apps on this list disclose selling health data. JabWell stores injection logs and AI chat locally on-device by default. Shotsy uses Apple Health framework which keeps data on-device unless you share it. Always review the privacy policy of any health app before entry of medication data — this ranking cannot substitute for your own due diligence on current privacy terms.
