Overview

How we ranked these apps

Search the App Store for "PCOS" and you get two kinds of apps: general period trackers that treat irregular cycles as an error, and diet programs that sell a plan. Very few do the unglamorous job of keeping your whole PCOS picture — cycles, symptoms, food, weight, photos and labs — in one record. This ranking covers seven apps worth installing in 2026 and who each one is actually for.

Across 7 apps we evaluated the features that matter to an actual user, not the ones that look good on a landing page:

  • Irregular cycle handling. Does the app accept long, absent or unpredictable cycles, or does it keep "predicting" a period that is not coming?
  • PCOS-specific tracking. Symptoms, acne and hair changes, weight, meals and supplements — not just flow.
  • Lab results. Can you store testosterone, SHBG, AMH, HbA1c and other panels next to your symptoms?
  • Fertility support. Fertile window estimates, ovulation test reading, BBT, TTC context.
  • Guidance quality. Lessons, coaching or an AI assistant — and whether it shows its sources.
  • Doctor usefulness. Can you export something a gynecologist or endocrinologist can read in two minutes?
  • Track record. Ratings, review volume and how long the app has existed.

None of these apps diagnose PCOS, prescribe treatment or replace a clinician. We say this once and assume it for the rest.

What none of these apps do

None of these apps — including ours — diagnose PCOS, prescribe metformin, inositol, spironolactone, hormonal contraception or fertility treatment, or replace a gynecologist, endocrinologist or reproductive specialist. They are tracking, education and program tools.

Fertile window predictions are less reliable when cycles are irregular. None of these apps is a contraceptive method unless it is specifically cleared for that use.

Absent periods for three months or more, very heavy bleeding, severe pelvic pain or symptoms of pregnancy complications need a clinician, not an app. In an emergency call 911 or your local emergency number.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why did you rank your own app first?

Cysta AI is the only app in this list that keeps cycles, symptoms, meals, weight, a photo journal and lab results in one record with an AI Coach over your own logs and a doctor-ready export. It is also the newest and least-reviewed app here — if track record matters most, Flo, Clue and Premom have millions of users.

What is the best free PCOS app?

Flo and Clue both offer free core cycle tracking. Most PCOS-specific apps, including Cysta AI, PCOS Pal and Cysterhood, put their main features behind a subscription or trial.

Can a PCOS app tell me if I have PCOS?

No. PCOS is diagnosed by a clinician after ruling out other causes. An app can help you record the cycle history and symptoms that conversation needs.

Which PCOS app is best for trying to conceive?

Premom if you use ovulation test strips and BBT. Cysta AI if you want TTC signs alongside symptoms and labs for a fertility consultation. Many people combine both.

Which PCOS app is best for weight loss?

Cysterhood and PCOS Pal are built as weight and nutrition programs with meal plans. Cysta AI logs meals and weight but does not prescribe a plan.
