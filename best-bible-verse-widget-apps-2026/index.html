Overview

How we chose these apps

A verse widget answers a simple wish: to see Scripture during the day without having to remember to open an app. Some apps do only that; others wrap the widget in reading plans, AI chat, community feeds and streaks. This guide covers seven iPhone apps worth considering in 2026 and who each one is really for.

We looked at what matters in daily use, not what looks good on a store page:

  • Where the verse shows up. Lock Screen, Home Screen, StandBy, and whether the full verse fits.
  • Where the words come from. A published Bible translation, word for word, or generated text.
  • Finding the right verse. Verses for how you feel, topics, or a fixed verse of the day.
  • Depth. The whole Bible, reading plans, audio, or just a daily verse.
  • Calm by design. Ads, feeds, chat, streaks and notifications, or the absence of them.
  • Price and track record. What is free, what is paid, and how many ratings the app has.

None of these apps replaces your church, your pastor or your own time in Scripture. App descriptions below are summarized from each app's public App Store listing at time of writing, October 2026.

YouVersion Bible App — Best Free Full Bible

What it is. The free Bible app from Life.Church: 3,500+ Bible versions in 2,300+ languages, thousands of reading plans, audio Bibles for select versions, prayer lists, videos, and notes and highlights that sync with a free account. Its listing says it has been downloaded over 700 million times.

Best for. Anyone who wants the whole Bible in their preferred translation, reading plans and friends to read with, at no cost.

Strengths. 4.9★ from about 13.8M ratings at time of writing, October 2026. Free. Huge choice of translations including NIV, ESV, NKJV, NLT and KJV. Offline reading for select versions.

Limitations. A full Bible platform rather than a widget app; a Lock Screen verse widget is not listed in its App Store description. With plans, videos, friends and prayer lists, there is a lot in one app.

Bible Widgets: Verses & Prayer — Best for Customizable Widgets

What it is. A widget-first app from Monkey Taps for the Lock Screen and Home Screen. Widgets can show Bible verses, prayers, Christian quotes or affirmations, over your own photo or a gallery background, with scheduled reminders and themed collections.

Best for. People who want widgets that mix verses with prayers and quotes, styled with their own photos.

Strengths. 4.9★ from about 117K ratings at time of writing, October 2026. Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets, custom backgrounds, collections, and offline access to saved content.

Limitations. Free with ads; Premium removes ads and unlocks unlimited reminders. The full Bible, audio and StandBy are not listed. Content categories include quotes and affirmations, not only Scripture.

Bible versions listed: NKJV, KJV, WMB, FBV, World English Bible, Amplified and NASB.

Limitations. Premium unlocks unlimited conversations and exclusive widget designs, with a 7-day free trial and then $4.99/week per its listing. A chat-centered app may not suit people who want Scripture only. StandBy is not listed.

Limitations. Premium (weekly or annual) adds audio plans, unlimited Bible Chat and an ad-free experience. Prayer streaks and a community feed may not suit people who want a quiet app. A Lock Screen widget is not listed.

What none of these apps do

None of these apps, including ours, replaces your church, a pastor or your own reading of Scripture in context. A widget is a reminder and a doorway, not a Bible study.

Many people use two apps: one for reading the whole Bible and following plans, and one for a verse on the Lock Screen. That is a perfectly good setup.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why is your own app listed first?

Two Sparrows is the only app in this list built around a full verse on the Lock Screen, Home Screen and StandBy with nothing else competing for attention: no ads, no feed, no chat and no streaks. It is also the newest app here, released in October 2026 with no ratings yet, and it has one translation. If you want the full Bible in many translations or a long track record, YouVersion is the stronger pick.

How do I put a Bible verse on my iPhone Lock Screen?

Install a verse widget app, then touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose the Lock Screen and add the app's widget. Two Sparrows walks you through this with a short video for each step. Bible Widgets, Bible Chat and Bible Path also list Lock Screen widgets.

What is the best free Bible app?

The YouVersion Bible App is free, with thousands of versions and reading plans, according to its App Store listing. #Bible - Verse of the Day is free to use with optional patronage subscriptions. Most other apps here are free to download with paid upgrades.

Which app shows Bible verses in StandBy?

Two Sparrows lists a StandBy view with a calm Candle look at night. StandBy is not listed in the App Store descriptions of the other apps in this guide.

Do these apps write Bible verses with AI?

Verse apps generally quote published translations. Two Sparrows states that every verse comes from the Berean Standard Bible, with no AI-written verses, and its "Scripture only" setting hides every AI feature. Bible Chat, Blessed and Bible Path include chat features; their answers are generated, while the verses they show come from the translations they list.

Which app helps when I feel anxious or can't sleep?

Two Sparrows lets you tap a feeling such as anxious, afraid or can't sleep and read verses that fit, with 7-day paths like Peace for anxious hearts and Sleep in peace. Blessed matches a morning verse and prayer to how you feel. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 in the US or your local emergency number.
