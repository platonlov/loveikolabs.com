Overview

How we ranked these apps

"Roofing app" covers three different jobs: calculators that turn your measurements into squares, materials and prices; measurement services that measure the roof for you from aerial imagery; and sales and business platforms. This ranking puts them side by side so you can pick the right tool — or the right two.

Across 7 apps we evaluated the features that matter to an actual user, not the ones that look good on a landing page:

  • Core math. Area, squares, pitch conversions and rafter lengths.
  • Materials and pricing. Bundles, underlayment, ridge cap, waste, labor and total job cost.
  • Measurement. Does the app measure the roof for you, or do you bring the numbers?
  • Job-site usability. Offline, readable in sunlight, usable with gloves.
  • Output. PDF estimates, reports or blueprints.
  • Cost. Free, paid per report, or subscription.

Roof work is dangerous — use fall protection. Calculators give estimates; verify against manufacturer specs and local code.

What none of these apps do

No app replaces a proper inspection, manufacturer installation instructions or local building code. Rafter sizing, spans and structural decisions should be checked against code tables or by a qualified professional.

Roof work is one of the most dangerous jobs in construction. Use fall protection and never work on wet, icy or steep roofs without training and equipment.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why did you rank your own app first?

Among roofing calculators, RoofingCalc Pro is the only one here that is completely free with no ads, account or data collection, works fully offline and covers area, materials, pitch, rafters, job cost and PDF export together. If you need the roof measured for you, GAF QuickMeasure or iRoofing do something RoofingCalc Pro doesn't.

What is the best free roofing calculator app?

RoofingCalc Pro — all features free. Roof Pitch Factor is also free but only measures pitch.

Which app measures a roof from satellite images?

GAF QuickMeasure (paid per report), iRoofing (subscription) and Pitch Gauge (map-based tracing). EagleView also sells aerial reports.

Which app is best for rafter calculations?

RedX Roof for full 3D framing and cut lists; RoofingCalc Pro or Construction Master Pro for quick common, hip and valley rafter lengths.

How do I calculate roofing squares?

Sloped roof area ÷ 100. Sloped area = footprint × slope factor (≈ 1.054 at 4/12, 1.118 at 6/12, 1.202 at 8/12, 1.414 at 12/12).

Squares, bundles, rafters and a price — free and offline.
