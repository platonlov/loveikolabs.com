The studio

A small studio with a narrow definition of done

Loveiko Labs was founded in 2024 by Valeriy Loveyko. It is an independent, self-funded iOS studio — no agency work, no investor roadmap, no feature padding.

Most apps try to do everything. We do the opposite. Every Loveiko Labs app starts with a single, specific, real-world question — is this gold real, is this medication safe in pregnancy, why is the baby crying, is this Rolex genuine — and ends when that question is answered clearly, with the next step the person can actually take. If a feature does not help answer the question, it does not ship.

That discipline is the whole studio. It lets a tiny team ship and refine twelve apps across jewelry, luxury resale, health, parenting, and personal style — each one competitive in its own category — without diluting any of them.

How we build

Principles we don't break

One job, done in 30 seconds

Each app solves one problem with an AI vision or audio workflow. Snap, scan, or record — get a structured, expert-level answer fast. No dashboards to learn.

Honest about limits

Our health and reference apps state plainly what they are not. No app here claims a certification, guarantee, or affiliation it does not have. Every product page carries explicit scope and limitation disclosures.

Grounded in real sources

Where an app touches health or authentication, its AI is grounded in publicly available reference material — NIH, FDA, RxNav, clinical society guidance, documented brand references — and it cites what it leans on.

Privacy by default

Camera and audio processing stays on-device or in a private request wherever possible. We collect what an app needs to work, and nothing we'd be uncomfortable explaining.
