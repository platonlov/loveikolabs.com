Best Handbag Authentication Apps and Services in 2026 — Honest Ranking

Seven tools cover most of the secondhand luxury handbag authentication market. They serve different buyers, charge wildly different prices, and produce different kinds of verdicts. Here's what each one is actually for.

We do not have an interest in being dishonest about the other six tools — dishonest comparisons drive readers to better-informed competitors and the category loses. How we ranked them

There is no single ranking that fits every buyer. A reseller listing 30 bags a month has different needs than someone bidding once on a $12,000 Birkin. So the tools below are ranked by category and matched to use cases.

The four dimensions that matter:

  • Verdict strength — does it produce a structured opinion, a certified verdict, or a written authentication letter?
  • Cost per check — what does it cost to verify one bag versus a hundred bags?
  • Speed — 30 seconds, 5 minutes, 24 hours, or 48 hours?
  • Brand coverage and depth — broad and shallow, or focused and deep?

1. Entrupy — the certified verdict

What it is: A microscopy-based authentication service founded in 2012. Uses a proprietary handheld device to capture material-level imaging, paired with their own AI. Issues an authentication certificate with a registry-verifiable serial number and a financial guarantee behind the verdict.

Strengths: The most legally and commercially recognized authentication output in the category. Certificate stands up to insurance claims, estate documentation, and high-stakes resale buyer demands. Microscopy reaches material details below what any phone camera can capture.

Limitations: Expensive at ~$99 per consumer scan and increasingly B2B-focused since 2022 — consumer access goes through partner store appointments rather than a direct app. Slow at consumer-scan volume; not designed for everyday pre-bid filtering.

Best for: Insurance claims. Legal disputes. Estate documentation. Bags worth over $5,000. Consignment business workflows. Buyers and sellers who need a verdict the world recognizes by serial number.

Pricing: ~$99 per consumer scan via partner stores; volume B2B pricing for businesses.

2. Real Authentication — the written letter

What it is: A human-expert photo review service for luxury handbags, watches, jewelry, and shoes. You submit detailed photos, a human authenticator reviews against their reference library, and you receive a written authentication letter.

Strengths: Real human authenticators with brand specialization. Written letter format works for many insurance and resale workflows. Covers 30+ brands including the major French and Italian houses. Recognized by some consignment stores.

Limitations: Slower than microscopy or AI tools — 12 to 48 hours per item depending on tier. Cost per check ($25–60) adds up at volume. Verdict quality depends on photo quality you submit.

Best for: Mid-range bags ($1,000–5,000) where a written letter matters more than a certificate. Brands Entrupy doesn't cover deeply. Vintage bags where human contextual judgment matters more than algorithm.

Pricing: ~$25–60 per item depending on turnaround tier.

3. VeriBag — the everyday reference check

What it is: An iPhone app that runs a 22-point AI reference check against documented public manufacturing patterns for Louis Vuitton, Chanel, Hermès, Gucci, and Prada. Built by Loveiko Labs (us) in 2026. Issues a structured consistency report and a shareable QR verify link — not a certificate.

Strengths: Fast (30 seconds). Cheap at volume ($7.99/wk unlimited, $39.99/yr unlimited). Supports uploading existing listing photos for pre-bid checks before you have the bag in hand. Shareable QR verify link works as a pre-ship listing certificate on Poshmark, Depop, Vestiaire, eBay, Mercari. Marks each checkpoint as consistent, flagged, or unclear, with cited public sources.

Limitations: No certificate, no financial guarantee, no legally-defensible verdict. Limited to five brands as of mid-2026 (LV, Chanel, Hermès, Gucci, Prada). Weaker on pre-1990 vintage. Requires internet for new scans.

Best for: Pre-bid checks on Vestiaire, The RealReal, eBay before placing the bid. Pre-ship listing certificates on Poshmark, Depop, Vestiaire (resellers pasting QR into listings). Gift-receiver private verification. Active resellers who run 5+ checks per week and need unlimited at flat cost. Bags under $5,000 in everyday secondhand workflows.

Pricing: $7.99/week (with 7-day free trial) · $39.99/year · $149.99 lifetime.

4. LegitApp — the generalist

What it is: A multi-category authentication app covering sneakers, watches, handbags, electronics, and other categories. AI-based photo analysis, broad but shallow per category.

Strengths: Covers multiple categories in one app — useful for resellers who flip sneakers, electronics, and handbags. Low per-check cost (~$1–5). Fast turnaround.

Limitations: Shallow per-brand depth. Handbag-specific checkpoints are less detailed than a focused tool like VeriBag. Some categories perform better than others — sneakers are a stronger LegitApp use case than luxury handbags.

Best for: Casual cross-category resellers. Single occasional checks on non-luxury or generalist items. Pairing with a focused tool (like VeriBag for handbags) when you also flip sneakers or electronics.

Pricing: ~$1–5 per check; subscription tiers available.

5. Bababebi — the vintage Hermès specialist

What it is: A boutique authentication service run by a single recognized authenticator specializing in vintage Hermès (Birkin, Kelly, Constance, and other archive references). Detailed individual review.

Strengths: Unmatched depth on vintage Hermès. Recognized name in the vintage Hermès collector community. Detailed reports that document specific era-marker analysis.

Limitations: Hermès only. Slower turnaround than scaled services. Higher cost per check appropriate for high-value vintage bags only.

Best for: Vintage Hermès Birkin, Kelly, and Constance authentication where the bag's value justifies specialist depth. Estate authentication for vintage Hermès collections. Pre-purchase due diligence on $10,000+ vintage Hermès.

Pricing: Varies by request; high per-item cost appropriate for high-value bags.

6. CheckCheck — the fast generalist

What it is: A fast multi-category photo authentication service originally popular in the sneaker market, expanded to handbags and other categories.

Strengths: Fast (typically under 24 hours, often under 1 hour). Low per-check cost. Strong sneaker-market reputation.

Limitations: Binary verdict format (authentic / not authentic) without structured checkpoint breakdown. Less depth on handbag-specific markers. Verdict-only output is harder to act on than a structured checklist.

Best for: Fast generalist checks where you need a quick yes/no rather than a detailed structured report. Pair with a structured tool when the bag is high-value.

Pricing: ~$5 per check.

7. Authenticate First — the established mail-in

What it is: An established mail-in and photo-based handbag authentication service with brand-specialist authenticators. Submits results as written verdicts with letterhead.

Strengths: Long-standing recognition in the luxury handbag community. Brand-specialist authenticators across Louis Vuitton, Chanel, Hermès, Gucci, Prada, and additional houses. Written verdict format.

Limitations: Slower than AI tools (24–72 hours). Cost per check adds up at volume. Mail-in workflow only practical for items in hand, not pre-bid checks.

Best for: Pre-resale verification of bags you already own. Buyer-paid authentication on Poshmark/Depop after purchase, before funds release. Mid-range to high-end transactions where a written authenticator's letter resolves disputes.

Pricing: ~$30–55 per item.

The honest summary

If you want a simple recommendation: for everyday secondhand luxury handbag activity in 2026, the most cost-effective workflow for most buyers and resellers is a combination — VeriBag as the primary reference layer (volume, speed, low cost, shareable QR for listings), Entrupy as the optional certificate layer for high-value transactions where a recognized written verdict matters.

If you only check one or two bags ever, you don't need a subscription — pay for one Real Authentication or Authenticate First review on that specific bag and you're done.

If you authenticate professionally at a consignment store, Entrupy's business workflow is the recognized standard and worth its cost.
