Overview

How we ranked these apps

Reflux apps have multiplied in 2026 — many new ones promise to "scan your food for reflux risk". A food scan is a start, but what actually helps is a record of meals and symptoms over weeks, and for silent reflux, a record of throat and voice symptoms too. These are the seven apps worth installing and who each is for.

Across 7 apps we evaluated the features that matter to an actual user, not the ones that look good on a landing page:

  • Meal and symptom logging. How quickly can you log a meal and how you felt afterwards?
  • Silent reflux (LPR) coverage. Throat clearing, hoarseness, cough, globus — or heartburn only?
  • Food guidance. Photo scanning, reference lists, or personalized trigger learning — and how honest it is about limits.
  • Insights. Charts, trigger ranking, AI assistant, and whether it uses your own records.
  • Doctor usefulness. Can you export a report for a GI or ENT appointment?
  • Privacy. Account requirements and how photos and text are processed.
  • Track record. Ratings, review volume and history.

None of these apps diagnose GERD or LPR, prescribe medication or replace a gastroenterologist or ENT. Chest pain can be cardiac — never self-manage it with an app.

What none of these apps do

None of these apps — including ours — diagnose GERD, LPR, esophagitis or Barrett's esophagus, prescribe PPIs, H2 blockers or alginates, or replace a gastroenterologist or ENT.

Chest pain can be a heart attack. Chest pain with shortness of breath, sweating, nausea or pain spreading to the arm, neck or jaw needs 911 or your local emergency number — not an app. Vomiting blood, black stools, difficulty swallowing and unintentional weight loss are warning signs that need prompt medical assessment.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why did you rank your own app first?

Refluxora is the only app here built around silent reflux (LPR) throat and voice symptoms as well as heartburn, and the only one that explains an existing lab report without guessing missing units or ranges. NoBurn has a larger, more established user base and a community — if that matters most, choose NoBurn.

What is the best app for silent reflux (LPR)?

Refluxora, because it records throat and voice symptom scores next to meals. Most reflux apps focus on heartburn, which many LPR sufferers barely have.

Can an app find my reflux triggers?

An app can help you find them by recording meals and symptoms over weeks. No app can determine your triggers from a single photo or a generic list.

Are free reflux apps any good?

Acid Reflux Diet Helper is a simple free reference. Most full trackers use a subscription or trial for their main features.

Should I use an app instead of seeing a doctor?

No. Use an app to prepare for the appointment. See a doctor for persistent symptoms, and seek emergency care for chest pain or signs of bleeding.
